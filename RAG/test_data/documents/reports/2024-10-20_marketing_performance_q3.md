## Executive Summary

Q3 2024 marked a pivotal quarter for marketing transformation, with strategic shifts paying off in measurable results. We executed on mid-market brand positioning, content quality improvements, and marketing-sales alignment initiatives launched in H1.

**Key Highlights**:
- ✅ **Pipeline Generated**: ¥310M (vs ¥180M in Q2, +72% QoQ)
- ✅ **SRL → Opportunity Conversion**: 38% (vs 12% in Q1, +26pp improvement)
- ✅ **Brand Awareness**: 28% unaided (vs 23% in Q2, target: 30% by EOY)
- ✅ **Marketing-Sourced Revenue**: ¥385M (43% of total new business)
- ✅ **Mid-Market Leads**: +47% QoQ (transformation momentum continues)

**Marketing Budget**: ¥42M (vs ¥40M guidance, -5% under budget)  
**CAC (Marketing portion)**: ¥1.8M per new customer (improving efficiency)  
**ROI**: 9.2:1 (¥385M revenue / ¥42M spend)

## 11. Lessons Learned

### 11.1 What Worked

1. **SRL Definition & Marketing-Sales Alignment** ✅
   - Lead quality 3x improvement (12% → 38% conversion)
   - Sales team satisfaction through the roof
   - Lesson: Alignment matters more than volume

2. **Content Quality Over Quantity** ✅
   - Research reports drive 52% conversion (vs 28% for blog posts)
   - Time on page up 240%
   - Lesson: One great piece beats 10 mediocre ones

3. **Mid-Market Brand Positioning** ✅
   - Mid-market awareness up 14pp
   - "Enterprise-only" perception down 8pp
   - Lesson: Brand repositioning takes time but works

4. **Data-Driven Optimization** ✅
   - Paid ads ROI up 29% via channel optimization
   - Cost per SRL down 18%
   - Lesson: Ruthlessly cut what doesn't work, double down on what does

### 11.2 What Didn't Work

1. **Events Strategy** ⚠️
   - ROI on events: 3.2:1 (vs 9.2:1 overall)
   - Lead quality from events lower than digital
   - Action: Reduce event spend in Q4, focus on select tier-1 events

2. **Display Advertising** ⚠️
   - ROAS: 6.5:1 (below average)
   - High cost per SRL (¥91K vs ¥78K avg)
   - Action: Pause display, reallocate to search

3. **Video Content (Learning)** 📊
   - Video series got views but conversion TBD
   - Too early to judge ROI (need 6 months data)
   - Action: Continue but measure closely

### 11.3 Key Takeaways

**For Marketing Team**:
- Quality metrics > vanity metrics
- Sales alignment is competitive advantage
- Long-form content performs best for B2B

**For Company**:
- Marketing ROI is strong (9.2:1) - continue investing
- Mid-market transformation succeeding
- Brand momentum building (stay the course)
