## 7. Unit Economics

### 7.1 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Calculation**: Total S&M Spend / New Customers Acquired

**Q2 2024 CAC**:
- S&M Spend: ¥380M
- New Customers: 37
- **CAC**: ¥10.3M per customer (includes marketing + sales fully loaded)

**Blended CAC (New + Expansion)**:
- S&M Spend: ¥380M
- New ARR (new + expansion): ¥323M
- **Blended CAC**: ¥1.18 (spend ¥1.18 to get ¥1 of new ARR)

**CAC Trend**:
- Q2 2023: ¥13.5M per customer
- Q1 2024: ¥11.8M per customer
- Q2 2024: ¥10.3M per customer (improving ✅)

### 7.2 Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Calculation**: (Average ARR × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate

**Q2 2024 LTV**:
- Average ARR per customer: ¥8.3M
- Gross Margin: 71.2%
- Annual Churn: 8.5%
- **LTV**: (¥8.3M × 71.2%) / 8.5% = **¥69.5M**

**Note**: LTV sensitive to churn rate. At 7% churn, LTV would be ¥84M.

### 7.3 LTV:CAC Ratio

**Q2 2024**: ¥69.5M (LTV) / ¥10.3M (CAC) = **6.7:1**

**Industry Benchmarks**:
- <3:1 = Unsustainable
- 3:1 to 5:1 = Healthy
- >5:1 = Excellent

**NexaTech Status**: Excellent (6.7:1)

### 7.4 CAC Payback Period

**Calculation**: CAC / (Average ARR × Gross Margin / 12 months)

**Q2 2024**:
- CAC: ¥10.3M
- Monthly Gross Profit per Customer: (¥8.3M × 71.2%) / 12 = ¥492K
- **Payback**: ¥10.3M / ¥492K = **21 months**

**Target**: <18 months  
**Status**: Slightly above target, improving trend

## 10. Risk Factors

### 10.1 Financial Risks

**1. DSO Deterioration** (HIGH PRIORITY)
- Risk: DSO increased from 45 → 78 days (¥120M tied up)
- Impact: Cash flow strain, runway reduction
- Mitigation: Working capital management program (DC_RAJ_005) - target 45 days by Dec 31

**2. Churn Increase**
- Risk: Churn increased from 6.0% → 8.5% annualized
- Impact: Reduces NRR, impacts growth, LTV:CAC deteriorates
- Mitigation: Customer success focus, product improvements, proactive outreach

**3. Sales Team Ramp**
- Risk: New AEs taking 6 months to ramp (vs 4 planned)
- Impact: Delayed revenue impact, lower productivity
- Mitigation: Improved training, mentorship, adjust expectations

**4. Economic Recession**
- Risk: Economic downturn impacts customer spending
- Impact: Slower growth, higher churn, deal slippage
- Mitigation: Strong balance sheet (18-month runway), diversified customer base

### 10.2 Operational Risks

**1. Competitive Pressure**
- Risk: WorkMax, EnterpriseFlow increasing aggression (pricing, feature parity)
- Impact: Win rate decline, pricing pressure
- Mitigation: Product differentiation (AI features), brand strength, customer relationships

**2. Security Incidents**
- Risk: Another security breach (post-DC_YUKI_005)
- Impact: Customer churn, regulatory penalties, brand damage
- Mitigation: Enhanced security posture, quarterly audits, bug bounty program

**3. Engineering Attrition**
- Risk: Loss of key technical talent (post-Hiroshi departure)
- Impact: Velocity decrease, knowledge loss, recruiting costs
- Mitigation: Career development program (DC_AKIKO_003), competitive comp, culture

**4. Execution Risks**
- Risk: Failure to deliver on roadmap (AI GA, performance, security)
- Impact: Customer dissatisfaction, churn, lost competitive advantage
- Mitigation: Technical debt addressed (DC_YUKI_004), clear priorities, resourced appropriately

### 10.3 Strategic Risks

**1. Market Consolidation**
- Risk: Larger players (Microsoft, Google) enter market
- Impact: Pricing pressure, feature competition, customer consolidation
- Mitigation: Focus on niche (B2B productivity), deep product, strong relationships

**2. International Expansion Timing**
- Risk: Competitors expand to SEA/APAC before us
- Impact: Lost market opportunity, harder entry later
- Mitigation: Monitoring closely, will expand when Japan dominance established

**3. Profitability vs Growth Trade-off**
- Risk: Slowing growth to reach profitability may reduce market position
- Impact: Competitors gain share, harder to fundraise later
- Mitigation: Balanced approach (25% growth + improving margins)
