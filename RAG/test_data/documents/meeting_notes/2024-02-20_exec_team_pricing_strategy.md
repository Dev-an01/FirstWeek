## 2. Financial Case for Price Increase (Raj Patel)

**Duration**: 2:20 - 2:50 PM

**Raj presented detailed financial model** (spreadsheet shared):

### Current State (Q4 2023)

- ARR: ¥4,150M
- Gross Margin: 68%
- Operating Margin: -15%
- Burn Rate: ¥155M/quarter

### Scenario Analysis

**Scenario A: No Price Change (Status Quo)**
- FY2024 Revenue: ¥5,000M (+20% growth from volume)
- Gross Margin: 68% (flat)
- Operating Margin: -12%
- Time to profitability: 8-10 quarters

**Scenario B: 10% Price Increase (New Customers Only)**
- FY2024 Revenue: ¥5,180M (+23% effective growth)
- Gross Margin: 70% (+2pp from leverage)
- Operating Margin: -9%
- Time to profitability: 6-8 quarters

**Scenario C: 15% Price Increase (New Customers Only)**
- FY2024 Revenue: ¥5,270M (+25% effective growth)
- Gross Margin: 71% (+3pp)
- Operating Margin: -6%
- Time to profitability: 5-7 quarters

**Scenario D: 10% Increase (All Customers, Including Renewals)**
- FY2024 Revenue: ¥5,565M (+34% growth)
- Gross Margin: 72% (+4pp)
- Operating Margin: -2%
- Time to profitability: 4 quarters
- **Risk**: High churn risk, customer dissatisfaction

### Raj's Recommendation

**Option: Scenario C (15% increase, new customers only)**

**Rationale**:
1. **Financial Impact**: +¥270M incremental revenue vs status quo
2. **Low Risk**: Existing customers not affected (maintain goodwill)
3. **Market Validation**: Competitors pricing higher (we have headroom)
4. **Value Justification**: Product improvements justify higher price
5. **Profitability**: Accelerates path to profitability by 3-4 quarters

**Implementation**:
- Effective: April 1, 2024 (start of Q2)
- New customers only (existing customers locked at current price)
- Existing customers: Increase only on renewal (case-by-case, with value adds)

### Financial Projections (Scenario C)

**Revenue Impact**:
- Q2 2024: +¥15M (15% of new sales)
- Q3 2024: +¥45M (cumulative effect)
- Q4 2024: +¥85M
- **FY 2024 Total**: +¥180M

**Churn Assumption**: 2-3% price-sensitive prospects walk away (acceptable)

**Q&A**:

**Kenji Sato** (Sales): "What if we lose 10% of prospects? That hurts."

**Raj**: "Fair concern. But math works even at 10% loss. Let me show..."

*Raj pulls up calculator:*

- Current: 100 prospects, 30% close rate, ¥85K price = ¥2,550M
- +15% price, -10% prospects: 90 prospects, 30% close rate, ¥98K price = ¥2,646M
- **Still +3.8% better revenue**

**Raj**: "We'd have to lose >20% of prospects to be worse off. Sarah's research says only 13% would reevaluate, and most of those still buy."

**Akiko**: "What about existing customer renewals? Are we raising prices there?"

**Raj**: "Not automatically. We hold current pricing for renewals unless customer demands more value (new features, more seats, etc.). Then we can price to value."

## 4. Customer Feedback & Risk Analysis (Miho Suzuki)

**Duration**: 3:10 - 3:30 PM

**Miho (VP Customer Success) presented customer sentiment analysis**:

### Customer Health Score

**Current customer base**:
- Green (healthy): 78%
- Yellow (at-risk): 18%
- Red (high churn risk): 4%

**For price increase risk analysis, we care most about renewals**:

### Renewal Cohort Analysis

**Customers renewing in next 12 months**: 245 customers (¥1,850M ARR)

**Segmented by price sensitivity**:

| Segment | % of ARR | Price Sensitivity | Churn Risk if +10% Price |
|---------|----------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Enterprise (Strategic) | 65% | Low | <5% |
| Mid-Market (Satisfied) | 25% | Medium | 10-15% |
| Small (Price-Conscious) | 10% | High | 20-30% |

**Miho's analysis**:
- Strategic accounts: Won't churn over 10% increase (we deliver too much value)
- Mid-market: Some churn risk, but mitigated if we hold price on renewal (only raise for new customers)
- Small customers: Highest risk, but smallest ARR impact

### Customer Interviews (Miho's team conducted 25)

**Question**: "If we raised prices 10-15%, how would you react?"

**Responses**:
- **Enterprise customers** (10 interviewed):
  - "We'd expect more value, but wouldn't leave" (7)
  - "We'd need to see roadmap justifying increase" (2)
  - "Would pressure us to review alternatives" (1)

- **Mid-market customers** (10 interviewed):
  - "Depends on how much more value you deliver" (6)
  - "We're happy, wouldn't churn over 10%" (3)
  - "We'd probably look at alternatives" (1)

- **Small customers** (5 interviewed):
  - "Price is important, but product works well" (2)
  - "We'd reevaluate all tools, might churn" (3)

### Miho's Recommendation

**Support price increase with conditions**:
1. **Hold prices on renewals** (new customers only pay increase)
2. **Add value for enterprise** (dedicated CSM, premium features)
3. **Small customer churn acceptable** (lowest NPS, highest support cost)

**Churn mitigation**:
- Announce price increase 90 days in advance (for new customers)
- Emphasize product improvements and roadmap
- Offer "lock in current pricing" for multi-year deals

**Q&A**:

**Akiko**: "What's your gut feeling? Will customers accept this?"

**Miho**: "Yes, especially if we're smart about communication. Customers respect transparency. If we say 'We've delivered 35% more value, price is going up 15%', they'll understand."

## Decision Made

**Akiko announced decision**:

"Okay, here's what we're doing:

**PRICING CHANGE (Effective April 1, 2024)**:

1. **New Customer Pricing** (+15%):
   - WorkFlow Pro Professional: ¥85K → ¥98K
   - TeamSync Professional: ¥45K → ¥52K
   - TalentHub Professional: ¥65K → ¥75K
   - Enterprise tiers: +10-15% (custom pricing)

2. **Existing Customers**:
   - Hold current pricing on renewals (locked in)
   - Exception: If customer adds significant value (new modules, 2x seats, etc.), we can reprice to market

3. **Grandfathering**:
   - In-flight deals (>50% through cycle): Honor old pricing
   - Signed but not started: Honor old pricing
   - 30-day grace period for prospects in late-stage discussions

4. **Communication**:
   - Internal announcement: March 1 (4 weeks lead time)
   - Customer announcement: March 15 (for transparency)
   - Sales enablement: March 1-15
   - Public pricing update: April 1

5. **Monitoring**:
   - Track win rate weekly (watch for drop)
   - Track churn monthly (existing customers)
   - Reassess in Q3 if unexpected issues arise

**This is strategic decision based on data. We've earned this increase through product improvements. Questions?"

### Concerns & Final Thoughts
