## 2. Financial Review Q2 (Raj Patel)

**Duration**: 10:20 - 10:50 AM

### Financial Performance

**Raj presented detailed financials** (see full memo: 2024-07-18_cfo_q2_financial_results.md)

**Key Metrics**:

| Metric | Q2 2024 | Q2 2023 | YoY | Target | vs Target |
|--------|---------|---------|-----|--------|-----------|
| Revenue | ¥1,285M | ¥1,042M | +23% | ¥1,250M | +3% ✅ |
| Gross Margin | 71% | 68% | +3pp | 70% | +1pp ✅ |
| OpEx | ¥1,010M | ¥895M | +13% | ¥1,050M | -4% ✅ |
| Operating Margin | -8% | -15% | +7pp | -10% | +2pp ✅ |
| Net Burn | ¥95M | ¥155M | -39% | ¥125M | -24% ✅ |
| Cash | ¥680M | ¥520M | +31% | ¥650M | +5% ✅ |

**Board reaction**: Very pleased with beat on all metrics.

### Key Discussion Points

**1. Gross Margin Improvement (68% → 71%)**

**Raj**: "Three drivers: AWS optimization (on track to ¥45M run-rate savings), pricing discipline (average discount down to 16%), and support efficiency."

**Yuki Kimura** (Independent Director): "71% is impressive. Can you sustain this?"

**Raj**: "Yes. AWS optimization has ¥12M more to go. We have pricing power (proposing 10-15% increase for new customers). Should maintain 70-72% range."

**2. DSO Concern (45 → 78 days)**

**Raj raised red flag**: "Days Sales Outstanding jumped from 45 to 78 days. This ties up ¥120M in working capital. I'm initiating comprehensive fix."

**Tomoko Sato** (Board Chair): "What's your action plan?"

**Raj**: 
- Tightening payment terms (default 30 days, not 45-60)
- Improving collections process (hiring 2 specialists)
- Adjusting sales comp (20% paid on cash collection within 45 days)
- Target: Back to 45 days within 6 months

**David Chen**: "Sales comp adjustment is smart. Aligns incentives."

**Board**: Approved DSO improvement plan.

**3. Path to Profitability**

**Raj**: "At current trajectory, we're 6-8 quarters from profitability. Break-even likely Q2 2025."

**Hiroshi Nakamura**: "Can you accelerate?"

**Raj**: "Three levers: (1) Revenue acceleration (hire more AEs), (2) Cost discipline (defer hiring), or (3) Pricing power (10-15% increase). I recommend option 3 - least disruptive, validates value prop."

**Board discussion**: Should we optimize for profitability or growth?

**Akiko**: "I believe we have pricing power. Our product has gotten significantly better (AI features, performance improvements). Customers are willing to pay more. Let's test with 10-15% increase for new customers, monitor closely."

**David Chen**: "Agree. Pricing is the best lever. Test and learn."

**Board vote**: Unanimous approval for pricing strategy (10-15% increase for new customers, effective Q3)

## 3. Product & Engineering Update (Yuki Nakamura)

**Duration**: 10:50 - 11:10 AM

### Product Development

**Yuki presented** (casual style, as expected):

"Hey team, quick update on what we shipped and what's coming. 🚀"

**Q2 Shipped**:
- AI search beta (WorkFlow Pro) - 200 users, getting great feedback
- Mobile app v2.1 - push notifications finally working 🎉
- Database migration - zero downtime (practiced 3x in staging first)
- TeamSync API v3 - better performance, new webhooks

**Q2 Tech Highlights**:
- Deploy frequency: 11/week (back to double digits!)
- Production bugs: 2 (down from 5 in Q1)
- Velocity: 87 story points/week (up 20% from Q1)

**Big Initiative: 6-Week Tech Debt Sprint** (July-August)
- "We accumulated tech debt. Need to pay it down before it kills us."
- Pausing new features for 6 weeks
- Focus: Tests, refactoring, security, performance
- **Opportunity cost**: ¥75M in deferred features
- **Expected payoff**: 30-50% velocity improvement long-term

**David Chen** (Sequoia): "Tech debt sprint is bold. Why now?"

**Yuki**: "Because we hit wall. Velocity down 40% from peak. Every new feature takes 2x longer. If we don't fix foundation, we'll slow to crawl. 6 weeks now saves 6 months later."

**Akiko**: "I support this. Sustainable velocity beats short-term features."

**Board discussion**: Some concern about 6-week feature freeze impact on sales.

**Kenji Sato** (VP Sales): "I'm actually fine with this. Sales team has plenty to sell. What we need is stability and performance. Customers complain about bugs more than missing features."

**Board vote**: Approved tech debt sprint with condition to report progress weekly.

### Engineering Team & Retention

**Yuki addressed Hiroshi's departure**:

"Real talk: Hiroshi leaving hurt. He's great, and I wished him well. But we didn't counter-offer 40% raise for good reasons."

**Rationale** (Akiko explained earlier, Yuki reinforced):
- Not sustainable to bid on every competitor offer
- Would set bad precedent
- Instead: Invested ¥5M in career development for whole team
- Promoted internal candidate (Yuki Tanaka) to VP Engineering

**Early results**:
- 3 senior engineers initially concerned, but stayed after career program announced
- Team morale improving (engagement survey +8 points)
- Recruiting improved (career development as selling point)

**Board**: Satisfied with approach.

## 5. Strategic Discussions

**Duration**: 11:30 AM - 12:00 PM

### Topic A: Fundraising Timing (DC_RAJ_004 discussion)

**Context**: Board asked whether to pursue Series C fundraising now.

**Raj presented analysis**:

**Case for raising now**:
- Market recovering (valuations improving post-2023 downturn)
- Strong Q2 results (good momentum story)
- War chest for aggressive growth

**Case for waiting**:
- Current runway: 18 months (not urgent)
- 6-8 quarters from profitability (can raise from strength)
- Valuation likely 30-40% higher if profitable
- Avoid dilution at less-than-optimal valuation

**Raj's recommendation**: "Wait 2-3 quarters. Get to break-even or clear path visible. Raise from position of strength."

**Board Discussion**:

**Hiroshi Nakamura** (SoftBank): "Market timing matters. If window closes, we may regret waiting."

**David Chen** (Sequoia): "Raj is right. You're too close to profitability to raise now. 18 months runway is plenty. Raise when you don't need money—that's when you get best terms."

**Tomoko Sato**: "I agree with David. Plus, Akiko, you've said you want to prove we can be profitable before raising again. This is your chance."

**Akiko**: "I'm comfortable waiting. We've been disciplined with capital. Let's get to break-even first, then raise from strength."

**Board vote**: 4-1 to defer fundraising (Hiroshi dissented, wanted to raise now for safety)

**Action**: 
- Engage investment bank on retainer (prepare materials, don't pitch)
- Cultivate investor relationships (warm conversations, not fundraising)
- Revisit in Q4 2024 or Q1 2025

### Topic B: Competitive Landscape

**Akiko**: "WorkMax is getting aggressive. They tried to poach Hiroshi (succeeded), tried to steal MegaCorp (failed), and are undercutting on price."

**Question to board**: "Should we respond with aggressive pricing, or stay disciplined?"

**David Chen**: "Never race to the bottom on price. You win on value and product. WorkMax has legacy tech and bad reputation for customer service. Stay the course."

**Yuki Nakamura** (CTO): "Our tech is better. AI features give us edge. WorkMax can't match on innovation."

**Sarah Kim**: "Our brand transformation is working. Mid-market sees us as more accessible than WorkMax. Don't get pulled into pricing war."

**Board consensus**: Stay disciplined on pricing, compete on product and brand.

### Topic C: International Expansion

**Hiroshi Nakamura**: "When do we expand beyond Japan? Southeast Asia? North America?"

**Akiko**: "Not yet. We're 80% focused on Japan. I want to dominate here first before spreading thin. Maybe 2025."

**Board**: Agreed. Focus before expansion.
