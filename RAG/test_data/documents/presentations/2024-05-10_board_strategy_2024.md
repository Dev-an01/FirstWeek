## SLIDE 8: Pillar 1 - Expand Market

**Go Beyond Enterprise**

### 1A: Mid-Market Transformation
Goal: 50% of customers from mid-market (100-499 employees) Today: 25% mid-market Strategy: Rebrand from "Enterprise-grade" to "Scales with you" Transparent pricing, self-serve options, case studies Investment: ¥25M (6 months), DC_SARAH_002 Timeline: Q1-Q2 2024 (in progress)


### 1B: Self-Serve / Product-Led Growth (PLG)
Goal: 20% of revenue from self-serve by 2026 Today: 0% (all sales-led) Strategy: Free trial, freemium tier, in-product conversion Investment: ¥40M (12 months) Timeline: Q4 2024 launch


### 1C: Asia-Pacific Expansion
Goal: 25% of revenue from APAC by 2026 (from 14% today) Today: Organic growth, no dedicated team Strategy: Localization, regional marketing, channel partners Investment: ¥80M (24 months) Timeline: 2025-2026 (not 2024 - focus Japan first)


---

## SLIDE 9: Pillar 2 - Accelerate Innovation

**Differentiate Through Product**

### 2A: AI-Powered Features
Goal: Be the AI-powered productivity platform Today: Beta with 200 users (DC_AKIKO_002) Strategy: AI search, recommendations, automation, insights Investment: ¥40M over 18 months (¥12M approved for pilot) Timeline: GA Q3 2024, expand Q4 2024 - 2025


### 2B: Mobile-First Experience

Goal: 50% of daily active users on mobile by 2026 Today: 22% on mobile (desktop-centric) Strategy: Rebuild mobile apps, offline mode, push notifications Investment: ¥30M (12 months) Timeline: Q3-Q4 2024


### 2C: Advanced Automation
Goal: Save customers 10+ hours/week per user Today: 5.2 hours/week Strategy: Workflow automation, integrations, no-code builder Investment: ¥25M (18 months) Timeline: 2025


---

## SLIDE 10: Pillar 3 - Build for Scale

**Strengthen Foundation**

### 3A: People Development (DC_AKIKO_003)
Problem: 12% attrition, career path unclear, lost VP Eng Solution: Career frameworks (IC + mgmt tracks) Mentorship, learning budgets, promotions Investment: ¥5M annually Timeline: Launched Q2 2024 Goal: <8% attrition, 30% internal promotions


### 3B: Process & Systems
Problem: Ad-hoc processes, manual work, scaling pains Solution: Standardized processes, automation, tooling Examples: Sales playbooks, CS workflows, finance automation Investment: ¥15M (12 months) Timeline: 2024-2025


### 3C: Leadership Team
Problem: VP Engineering gap, leadership depth Solution: Hire 2-3 senior leaders (VP Product, VP Eng, CRO?) Develop high-potential managers Investment: ¥50M (new hires + development) Timeline: 2024-2025
