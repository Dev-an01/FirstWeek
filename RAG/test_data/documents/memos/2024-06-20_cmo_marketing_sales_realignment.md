---

## Root Cause Analysis

I spent last week digging into this. Here's what I found:

### 1. Bad MQL Definition

**Current MQL criteria** (too loose):
- Downloaded 2 pieces of content **OR**
- Attended 1 webinar **OR**
- Visited pricing page 3x

**Problem**: Activity ≠ buying intent
- Students downloading whitepapers for school projects = MQL
- Competitors researching us = MQL
- Tire-kickers with zero budget = MQL

### 2. Misaligned KPIs

**Marketing rewarded for**: MQL volume  
**Sales rewarded for**: Closed deals

**Result**: Marketing optimizes for quantity (easy to hit 1,200 MQLs). Sales ignores them (because 88% are junk). Marketing celebrates. Sales suffers.

**We're not on the same team. We're playing different games.**

### 3. No Feedback Loop

**Marketing doesn't know**:
- Which MQLs became customers
- Which campaigns actually work
- What "good" looks like

**Sales doesn't tell us**:
- Why leads are rejected
- What they actually need
- Which sources are valuable

**We're flying blind.**

### 4. Broken Hand-off

**Current process**:
1. Marketing dumps leads in Salesforce
2. Sales picks them up... eventually... maybe
3. No SLA, no accountability
4. Leads go cold

**18-hour average response time** is unacceptable. Should be <2 hours.

## What We're Changing (Effective Immediately)

### CHANGE #1: New Lead Definition

**OUT**: MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)  
**IN**: SRL (Sales-Ready Lead)

**NEW SRL Criteria** (much tighter):

Must have **ALL** of:
1. Works at target company size (100+ employees)
2. Job title indicates decision influence
3. **Engaged with bottom-funnel content**:
   - Downloaded ROI calculator **OR**
   - Watched product demo video **OR**
   - Requested demo **OR**
   - Visited pricing 5+ times in 7 days

**Why this works**: These are buying signals, not just activity.

### CHANGE #2: New Marketing KPIs

**OLD KPIs** (Volume-based):
- MQL count: 1,200/quarter ❌
- Content downloads ❌
- Website traffic ❌

**NEW KPIs** (Quality-based):
- **SRLs generated**: 400/quarter (target)
- **SRL → Opportunity conversion**: 35%+ (target)
- **Pipeline generated**: ¥310M/quarter (target)
- **Revenue influenced**: Track to closed-won

**I'm personally accountable for pipeline, not just lead volume.**

### CHANGE #3: Bi-Directional SLAs

**Marketing SLA**:
- Deliver 400 SRLs/quarter (down from 1,200 MQLs)
- 35%+ conversion to opportunities
- Weekly quality reviews with sales

**Sales SLA**:
- Contact SRLs within 2 hours (down from 18 hours)
- Provide feedback on lead quality (weekly)
- Update lead status within 48 hours

**Both teams accountable. No more blame game.**

### CHANGE #4: Weekly Sync (Revenue Team Meeting)

**New weekly meeting** (Fridays 10 AM, 30 min):
- Review SRL quality
- Discuss which campaigns working/not working
- Align on next week priorities
- Celebrate wins together

**Attendees**:
- Me (Sarah)
- VP Sales (Kenji)
- Demand Gen team (3 people)
- Sales leaders (3 people)

**Purpose**: One team, one goal (revenue)

### CHANGE #5: Closed-Loop Reporting

**New monthly report** (Finance + Marketing + Sales):
- Which SRLs became customers?
- Which campaigns drove closed-won revenue?
- What's the ROI by channel?
- Where should we invest more/less?

**This is Marketing 101. We somehow forgot it.**

## Why This Will Work

### 1. Quality Over Quantity

**400 SRLs at 35% conversion** = 140 opportunities  
**1,200 MQLs at 12% conversion** = 144 opportunities

**Same opportunity volume, but**:
- ¥22M / 400 = ¥55K per quality lead (vs ¥153K before)
- Sales spends 82% of time on quality (vs 12% before)
- Marketing focuses on bottom-funnel (high-intent)

### 2. Aligned Incentives

**Both teams now measured on**:
- Pipeline generated
- Revenue influenced

**Success looks like**:
- Marketing delivers fewer but better leads
- Sales converts them at higher rate
- Together we hit revenue targets

### 3. Faster Feedback

**Weekly syncs mean**:
- Marketing learns what's working immediately (not quarterly)
- Sales provides input on lead quality real-time
- We adjust campaigns weekly (not quarterly)

**Agile marketing + sales = faster learning**
