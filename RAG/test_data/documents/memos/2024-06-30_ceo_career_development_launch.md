## A Letter to the Team

Team,

Three months ago, we faced a difficult moment. Hiroshi Yamada, our VP of Engineering, accepted an offer from WorkMax with a 40% salary increase. While we're happy for Hiroshi's opportunity, his departure raised important questions:

**Are we doing enough to support your career growth?**

The answer was: Not enough.

## What We Learned

After Hiroshi left, three senior engineers expressed concerns about career growth at NexaTech. This was a wake-up call. I spent the last 90 days:

- Speaking with 40+ employees about career aspirations
- Analyzing our promotion data (only 15% of roles filled internally)
- Benchmarking against companies known for great career development
- Listening to what YOU told us in the quarterly survey

Your message was clear: **You want to grow here, but the path isn't always clear.**

## Our Commitment: Investment in Your Growth

Today, I'm announcing a comprehensive Career Development Program—our largest investment in people development to date.

### 1. Clear Career Paths (IC and Management Tracks)

**No more "up or out"**—you can grow as:
- **Individual Contributor (IC)**: Junior → Mid → Senior → Staff → Principal
- **Management Track**: Manager → Senior Manager → Director → VP → C-Level

Both tracks have:
- ✅ Equivalent compensation at same levels
- ✅ Clear competency requirements
- ✅ Expected impact and scope
- ✅ Promotion criteria (no more guessing)

**New career frameworks** published today on internal site: [careers.nexatech.jp]

### 2. Increased Learning Budget

**Effective immediately**:
- Individual Contributors: ¥150K → ¥150K (maintained)
- Senior IC / Managers: ¥250K → ¥250K (maintained)
- Directors / VPs: ¥400K → ¥400K (maintained)

**Plus new benefits**:
- Unlimited professional books (no budget limit)
- Full certification costs covered (AWS, PMP, CISSP, etc.)
- Degree program support: Up to ¥2M for MBA/MS programs

### 3. Mentorship Program

Starting Q3:
- 6-month formal mentorship cohorts
- Mentees matched with senior leaders (different teams)
- Structured curriculum + open discussions
- 50 spots in first cohort (apply by July 15)

### 4. Internal Mobility

**We want you to explore**:
- Job shadowing across departments
- Rotational programs (engineering, product, sales)
- Internal applications get priority (posted internally 5 days before external)

### 5. Quarterly Career Conversations

**Managers will hold quarterly 1:1s** focused on:
- Your career aspirations
- Skills you want to develop
- Stretch assignments
- Promotion readiness

These are separate from performance reviews—purely developmental.

## Why This Matters

I could have counter-offered Hiroshi with more money. But money alone doesn't solve the real issue: **You need to see a future here.**

Our industry moves fast. Skills from 3 years ago are table stakes today. If we don't invest in your growth, we're asking you to stagnate. That's not fair to you, and it's not smart for us.

**Great companies are built by people who grow together.**

## The Business Case

Some might ask: "Can we afford this?"

The better question: **Can we afford NOT to?**

- Replacing a senior engineer: ¥15M+ (recruiting + ramp time)
- Three engineers left post-Hiroshi: ¥45M+ in replacement cost
- Career development program: ¥5M annually
- **ROI: 9:1 if we retain just 3 people per year**

Plus intangibles:
- Knowledge retention
- Stronger culture
- Better recruiting (people want to join companies that invest in growth)

## What Success Looks Like

In 12 months, I want to see:
- **30% of roles filled internally** (up from 15%)
- **Employee satisfaction with career growth**: >8/10 (currently 6.1/10)
- **Retention of high performers**: >95% (currently 88%)
- **Clear career visibility**: Every employee knows their next step

## Your Role

**This program only works if YOU engage**:
- Own your career (we provide resources, you drive)
- Have honest conversations with your manager
- Take advantage of learning budget
- Apply for mentorship, rotations, internal roles
- Give feedback (we'll adjust based on what works)

## A Personal Note

I co-founded NexaTech because I believe work should be fulfilling. Part of fulfillment is growth—becoming better at your craft, expanding your impact, achieving your potential.

**We want to be the place where you do the best work of your career.**

Not because we trap you here, but because you **choose** to stay because you're growing.
