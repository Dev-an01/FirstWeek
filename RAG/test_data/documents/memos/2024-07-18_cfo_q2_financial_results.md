## Pricing Strategy Discussion

### Context

Q2 saw pricing pressure in several large deals:
- 3 enterprise prospects requested >20% discounts
- 2 customers threatened churn over price increases
- Competitive landscape intensifying (WorkMax, EnterpriseFlow)

### Decision Framework (DC_RAJ_002)

Rather than reactively discount, I'm proposing **strategic price increases** for new customers:

**Rationale**:
1. Our value proposition strengthened (new AI features, better performance)
2. Gross margin room (71% vs 70% target)
3. Customer willingness to pay validated (low churn despite economic uncertainty)

**Proposed increases** (effective Q3):
- WorkFlow Pro Professional: ¥85K → ¥95K (+12%)
- TeamSync Professional: ¥45K → ¥52K (+16%)
- Enterprise tiers: +10% across board

**Expected impact**:
- Revenue uplift: ¥180M annually
- Customer pushback risk: 10-15% of prospects (acceptable)
- Competitive positioning: Still 15% below WorkMax

**This requires CEO approval** - recommending we proceed.

## Fundraising Consideration (DC_RAJ_004)

### Board Question: "Should we raise Series C now?"

**My recommendation: NO, not yet.**

**Analysis**:

**Pros of raising now**:
- Market recovering (valuations improving)
- Strong Q2 results (good story)
- War chest for growth

**Cons of raising now**:
- We have 18 months runway (not urgent)
- 2-3 quarters from profitability
- Can raise from position of strength (profitable) vs need (burning cash)
- Dilution at current valuation vs future valuation

**Better timing**: Q4 2024 or Q1 2025
- Either break-even or clear path visible
- Another 2 quarters of 25%+ growth
- Valuation 30-40% higher

**Recommendation**: 
- Defer fundraising for 2-3 quarters
- Focus on path to profitability
- Engage investment bank on retainer (prepare but don't execute)
- Cultivate investors (warm relationships, don't pitch yet)
