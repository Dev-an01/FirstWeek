## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes time off benefits to:
- Support work-life balance and well-being
- Comply with Japan labor law
- Provide flexibility for personal needs
- Encourage rest and recharge

## 2. Scope

Applies to all regular full-time NexaTech employees

**Part-time employees**: Prorated based on working hours

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 3.1 Annual PTO Allowance

**Accrual based on tenure**:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Monthly Accrual |
|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Year 1 | 15 days | 1.25 days/month |
| Years 2-5 | 20 days | 1.67 days/month |
| Years 6-10 | 22 days | 1.83 days/month |
| Years 11+ | 25 days | 2.08 days/month |

**Accrual method**: Monthly on first day of month (prorated for mid-month starts)

**Example**: Employee starting June 15 gets 0.63 days PTO for June (1.25 × 15/30 days)

### 3.2 PTO Usage

**Minimum increment**: Half day (4 hours) or full day (8 hours)

**Advance notice**:
- <3 days PTO: 1 week advance notice
- 3-5 days PTO: 2 weeks advance notice
- >5 days PTO: 4 weeks advance notice (for proper coverage)

**Blackout periods**: None company-wide, but teams may have busy periods (discuss with manager)

**Manager approval**: Required for all PTO requests

### 3.3 PTO Rollover

**Year-end rollover**: Maximum 5 days to next year

**Unused PTO**: Forfeit after 1 year (use it or lose it)

**Rationale**: Encourage employees to actually take time off

### 3.4 PTO Payout

**At termination**: Unused accrued PTO paid out

**Cannot cash out** during employment (must use for time off)

## 5. Sick Leave

### 5.1 Sick Leave Allowance

**10 days paid sick leave** per year (separate from PTO)

**Accrual**: Available from day 1 (not accrued monthly)

**Rollover**: Does not roll over to next year

**Medical certification**:
- <3 days: No doctor's note required
- ≥3 days: Doctor's note required
- Extended illness: Doctor's note + periodic updates

### 5.2 Using Sick Leave

**When to use**:
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments
- Mental health days (no stigma)
- Recovery from medical procedures

**Notification**:
- Contact manager ASAP (before start of workday)
- Slack or email acceptable
- Update team/manager on expected return

**No penalty**: We trust employees, no questions asked for sick leave

### 5.3 Extended Illness

**If sick >10 days in year**:
- Can use PTO for additional days
- Or unpaid leave (see Section 9)
- Long-term disability insurance available (if >30 days)

**Serious illness**: Discuss with HR for accommodation options

## 6. Parental Leave

### 6.1 Maternity Leave

**Eligibility**: Pregnant employees

**Duration**:
- 6 weeks before due date (if requested)
- 8 weeks after birth (mandatory under Japan law)
- **Total**: Up to 14 weeks

**Pay**: Full salary (100% paid by company)

**Job protection**: Guaranteed return to same or equivalent role

### 6.2 Paternity Leave

**Eligibility**: All new fathers (including adoptive)

**Duration**: 8 weeks (within 1 year of birth)

**Pay**: Full salary (100% paid by company)

**Flexible**: Can take continuously or split into 2 periods

### 6.3 Parental Leave (Childcare Leave)

**Eligibility**: All parents after maternity/paternity leave

**Duration**: Up to 1 year per child (can extend to 18 months in special cases)

**Pay**: 
- First 6 months: 67% of salary (government subsidy)
- Months 7-12: 50% of salary (government subsidy)
- Company does not top-up (follows Japan standard)

**Job protection**: Guaranteed return to same or equivalent role

**Flexible return**: Can return earlier or extend with manager discussion

### 6.4 Adoption Leave

**Same benefits** as maternity/paternity leave

**Duration**: 8 weeks full-paid leave upon adoption

## 7. Bereavement Leave

### 7.1 Immediate Family

**Definition**: Spouse, children, parents, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren

**Duration**: 5 days paid leave

**Flexibility**: Can take non-consecutively within 3 months of death

**Travel**: If overseas funeral, additional 2 days travel time (paid)

### 7.2 Extended Family

**Definition**: Aunts, uncles, cousins, in-laws

**Duration**: 3 days paid leave

### 7.3 Funeral Assistance

**Company provides**:
- Flowers/wreath for funeral
- Support with arrangements (if needed)
- Employee Assistance Program (counseling)

## 8. Other Types of Leave

### 8.1 Jury Duty / Court Appearance

**Pay**: Full salary (treated as work day)

**Duration**: As required

**Process**: Provide court summons to manager and HR

### 8.2 Voting

**Japan election day**: Company encourages voting
- Flexible schedule to vote
- Up to 2 hours paid time if needed

### 8.3 Volunteer Time Off (VTO)

**Annual allowance**: 2 days paid VTO

**Eligible activities**:
- Approved charitable organizations
- Community service
- Disaster relief

**Approval**: Manager + HR (ensure organization is legitimate)

**Team volunteer**: Company organizes quarterly volunteer day (separate from individual VTO)

### 8.4 Education/Exam Leave

**For job-relevant certifications**:
- Up to 2 days paid leave for exam preparation
- Exam day: Paid time off

**See**: Professional Development Policy (POLICY-HR-003)

## 9. Unpaid Leave

### 9.1 Personal Leave of Absence

**Eligibility**: Employees with 1+ year tenure

**Duration**: Up to 3 months (with manager and HR approval)

**Reasons**:
- Extended travel
- Family care beyond parental leave
- Personal circumstances
- Sabbatical

**Benefits during leave**:
- Health insurance maintained (employee pays premium)
- No salary
- PTO does not accrue
- Job protection (but not guaranteed same role)

**Return**: 4 weeks advance notice required

### 9.2 Medical Leave of Absence

**Eligibility**: All employees

**Duration**: Up to 6 months

**Process**:
- Doctor's certification required
- Periodic medical updates
- HR coordinates accommodations
- Return-to-work clearance needed

**Benefits**: Health insurance maintained (company pays)

## 10. Leave Request Process

### 10.1 How to Request

**PTO and planned leave**:
1. Check team calendar for conflicts
2. Request via HR system (BambooHR)
3. Manager receives notification
4. Approval within 48 hours
5. Add to team calendar

**Sick leave**:
1. Message manager ASAP (Slack/email)
2. Log in HR system when able
3. No pre-approval needed

**Extended leave** (parental, sabbatical):
1. Discuss with manager (as early as possible)
2. Submit formal request to HR
3. HR coordinates paperwork
4. Transition plan created

### 10.2 Manager Responsibilities

**Reviewing requests**:
- Consider business needs and coverage
- Can request alternative dates (with reason)
- Cannot deny without valid business reason
- Encourage employees to take PTO

**Valid denial reasons**:
- Critical project deadline
- Multiple team members already off
- Insufficient coverage

**Must approve**:
- Sick leave (always)
- Bereavement leave (always)
- Parental leave (legal right)

### 10.3 Coverage Planning

**Before taking leave**:
- Document work status
- Delegate responsibilities
- Set up out-of-office messages
- Provide emergency contact info (if applicable)

**During leave**:
- Disconnect from work (we mean it!)
- No expectation to check email/Slack
- Emergency only contacts honored

## 11. Work-Life Balance Practices

### 11.1 Encouraged Time Off

**Minimum expectation**: Take at least 10 days PTO per year

**Manager check-ins**: If employee not taking PTO, manager discusses why

**Forced PTO**: If employee has >20 unused days approaching year-end, HR may require minimum usage

**Rationale**: Burnout prevention, healthy sustainable work culture

### 11.2 No "Hero" Culture

**We do NOT reward**:
- Never taking PTO
- Working while sick
- Skipping parental leave
- Answering emails on vacation

**We DO reward**:
- Sustainable high performance
- Work-life integration
- Taking care of yourself

### 11.3 Leadership by Example

**Executives and managers expected to**:
- Take their full PTO allocation
- Actually disconnect on vacation
- Respect others' time off
- Model healthy boundaries

## 14. Time Off and Performance

### 14.1 No Negative Impact

**Taking time off** does NOT negatively impact:
- Performance reviews
- Promotion decisions
- Project assignments
- Manager perception

**In fact**: Taking appropriate time off seen as positive (self-care, sustainable performance)

### 14.2 Extended Absence Impact

**If extended leave** (>1 month):
- May affect annual goals (will be adjusted)
- May affect project timelines (understandable)
- Will not affect career progression unfairly

**Manager responsibility**: Adjust expectations fairly for employees returning from leave

## 15. Special Circumstances

### 15.1 Natural Disasters

**If natural disaster prevents work**:
- Paid time off (not counted against PTO)
- Safety first, no penalty
- Work from home if possible

**Recent precedent**: March 2024 earthquake - employees affected given 3 days special paid leave

### 15.2 Pandemic/Health Crises

**If government mandates quarantine/lockdown**:
- Remote work if possible
- If can't work remotely: Paid leave (special category)
- No PTO deduction

**COVID-19 precedent**: Employees quarantined received full pay, no PTO used

### 15.3 Company Discretionary Leave

**CEO can grant special paid leave** for:
- Company-wide celebrations (IPO, major milestone)
- Employee life events (wedding, etc.)
- Other circumstances

**Recent example**: December 2023 - Company hit ¥5B revenue target, CEO gave everyone extra 2 days off

## 17. Policy Violations

### 17.1 Misuse of Leave

**Prohibited**:
- ❌ Claiming sick leave when not sick
- ❌ Working for another company during leave
- ❌ Misrepresenting reason for leave
- ❌ Fraudulent medical documentation

**Consequences**: Disciplinary action up to termination

### 17.2 Manager Violations

**Prohibited**:
- ❌ Denying legitimate leave requests
- ❌ Pressuring employees not to take leave
- ❌ Contacting employees during leave (non-emergency)
- ❌ Penalizing employees for taking leave

**Consequences**: Manager performance review issue, training required

**To report**: HR or anonymous hotline
