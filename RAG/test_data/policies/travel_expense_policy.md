## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for business travel expenses to ensure:
- Cost-effective travel arrangements
- Consistent expense standards across organization
- Timely reimbursement for employees
- Compliance with tax regulations

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all NexaTech employees traveling for business purposes including:
- Customer meetings and conferences
- Sales calls and prospecting
- Partner meetings
- Company offsites and events
- Training and professional development

---

## 3. Travel Approval

### 3.1 Domestic Travel (within Japan)

| Purpose | Approval Required |
|---------|------------------|
| Customer meetings | Manager approval |
| Conferences (registration <¥50K) | Manager approval |
| Conferences (registration ≥¥50K) | Department head approval |
| Team offsites | Department head approval |

### 3.2 International Travel

| Purpose | Approval Required |
|---------|------------------|
| Customer meetings | VP + CFO approval |
| Conferences | VP + CFO approval |
| Sales prospects | VP Sales + CFO approval |
| Executive travel | CEO approval |

**Advance notice**: International travel requires 14 days advance approval except emergencies.

---

## 4. Transportation

### 4.1 Air Travel

**Class of Service**:
- **Domestic flights**: Economy class
- **International flights <6 hours**: Economy class
- **International flights ≥6 hours**: Premium economy (VP and above) or Economy (all others)
- **International flights ≥12 hours**: Business class (C-level only)

**Booking**:
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Use approved travel agency or company booking tool
- Choose lowest logical airfare considering:
  - Schedule efficiency
  - Connection times
  - Total travel time

**Exceptions**: 
- Medical conditions requiring upgrade: Manager + HR approval
- Emergency travel: VP approval

### 4.2 Rail Travel (Shinkansen, etc.)

- **Green Car**: Allowed for travel >2 hours
- **Ordinary Car**: Standard for travel <2 hours
- Book Reserved seats when available

### 4.3 Ground Transportation

**Taxis/Ride-sharing**:
- Allowed when public transportation is impractical
- Required receipts for all rides
- Pool with colleagues when possible

**Rental Cars**:
- Economy or compact class
- Requires manager pre-approval
- Include insurance coverage
- Gas receipts required

**Personal Vehicle**:
- Reimbursement: ¥20/km
- Parking receipts required
- Insurance coverage confirmation required

---

## 5. Accommodations

### 5.1 Hotel Standards

| Employee Level | Max Per Night (¥) - Tokyo/Major Cities | Max Per Night (¥) - Other Cities |
|----------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Individual Contributor | 18,000 | 12,000 |
| Manager | 22,000 | 15,000 |
| Director/VP | 28,000 | 20,000 |
| C-Level | 35,000 | 25,000 |

**Guidelines**:
- Book hotels within reasonable distance of meeting location
- Use corporate-rate hotels when available
- Extended stays (>5 nights): Negotiate weekly rate

**Exceptions**:
- Conference hotel (if conference venue): Allowed at conference rate
- Peak season/limited availability: VP approval for exceeding limits

---

## 6. Meals and Entertainment

### 6.1 Meals (Per Diem)

When traveling, per diem allowance (no receipts required under limit):

| Meal | Domestic (¥) | International (¥) |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| Breakfast | 1,500 | 2,000 |
| Lunch | 2,000 | 2,500 |
| Dinner | 3,500 | 5,000 |
| **Daily Total** | **7,000** | **9,500** |

**Notes**:
- If hotel includes breakfast, deduct ¥1,500 from per diem
- Receipts required for meals exceeding per diem
- Alcohol: Max ¥3,000/day included in dinner allowance

### 6.2 Client Entertainment

**Limits**:
- Sales meals with prospects: ¥15,000 per person
- Executive client dinners: ¥25,000 per person
- Client entertainment (events, activities): Requires VP approval

**Requirements**:
- Receipt required
- Document attendees and business purpose
- No entertainment without business discussion

---

## 7. Conference and Event Expenses

### 7.1 Conference Registration

**Approval Limits**:
- <¥50,000: Manager approval
- ¥50,000 - ¥150,000: Department head approval
- >¥150,000: VP + CFO approval

**Early bird discounts**: Book early when available (typically 20-30% savings)

### 7.2 Conference Travel

When attending conferences:
- Hotel: Conference hotel rate allowed (even if exceeds policy limits)
- Meals: Per diem + reasonable conference meals
- Networking events: Included in conference purpose

---

## 8. Expense Reporting

### 8.1 Submission Timeline

- Submit expense reports within **14 days** of trip completion
- Monthly travelers: Submit by 5th of following month
- Reimbursement: Within 15 business days of approval

### 8.2 Required Documentation

For each expense:
- ☐ Original itemized receipt (photo acceptable)
- ☐ Business purpose description
- ☐ Attendees (for meals/entertainment)
- ☐ Mileage log (for personal vehicle)
- ☐ Foreign currency exchange rate (for international)

### 8.3 Expense Categories

Use correct categories in expense system:
- **TRAVEL-AIR**: Airfare
- **TRAVEL-RAIL**: Train/Shinkansen
- **TRAVEL-GROUND**: Taxi, rental car, parking
- **TRAVEL-HOTEL**: Lodging
- **TRAVEL-MEALS**: Meals (per diem or actual)
- **TRAVEL-CONFERENCE**: Registration fees
- **TRAVEL-CLIENT**: Client entertainment
- **TRAVEL-OTHER**: Misc (internet, baggage fees, etc.)

---

## 9. Prohibited Expenses

The following are **NOT reimbursable**:
- ❌ Personal travel expenses
- ❌ Spouse/family travel (unless approved by CEO)
- ❌ In-room entertainment (movies, minibar)
- ❌ Personal grooming (haircuts, spa)
- ❌ Traffic violations or parking fines
- ❌ Lost baggage or personal items
- ❌ Travel insurance (company policy covers business travel)
- ❌ Commuting to/from regular office

---

## 10. International Travel Considerations

### 10.1 Currency Exchange

- Use corporate credit card for charges (best exchange rate)
- If using personal funds, reimburse at rate on transaction date
- Attach screenshot of exchange rate from xe.com or equivalent

### 10.2 Visas and Vaccinations

- Visa fees: Reimbursable for business travel
- Required vaccinations: Reimbursable with medical receipt
- Passport fees: Not reimbursable (personal document)

### 10.3 Travel Insurance

- Company policy covers international business travel
- Submit claims to HR for medical issues abroad
- Travel delay/cancellation covered by corporate credit card

---

## 11. Corporate Credit Cards

### 11.1 Eligibility

Corporate cards issued to:
- All employees who travel monthly
- Directors and above (regardless of travel frequency)
- Others by exception (CFO approval)

### 11.2 Usage

**Authorized**:
- Business travel expenses
- Client entertainment (within policy)
- Business supplies/equipment
- Conference registrations

**Prohibited**:
- Personal purchases
- Cash advances
- Lending to colleagues
- Purchases for non-employees

### 11.3 Reconciliation

- Review and approve charges within 5 business days
- Attach receipts for all charges >¥5,000
- Dispute charges immediately with provider

---

## 12. Frequent Flyer and Hotel Points

- **Ownership**: Points earned on business travel belong to employee
- **Usage**: May use for personal travel
- **Upgrades**: May use points to upgrade business class (at your discretion)

**Company benefits**:
- Use corporate membership numbers when available
- Negotiated corporate rates benefit company

---

## 13. Travel Advances

### 13.1 Eligibility

Travel advances available for:
- International travel >¥100,000 estimated expenses
- Extended travel (>7 days)
- Employees without corporate credit card

### 13.2 Process

1. Request advance 10 days before travel via expense system
2. Manager approval required
3. Finance issues advance (wire or check)
4. Settle within 14 days post-travel (refund excess or claim additional)

---

## 14. Exceptions and Special Circumstances

### 14.1 Executive Travel

C-level executives may exceed standard limits when:
- Representing company at high-level meetings
- Extended international travel (>1 week)
- Security or privacy concerns

Approval: CEO (or Board for CEO travel)

### 14.2 Emergency Travel

For emergencies (family, urgent customer issue):
- Book immediately, document as emergency
- Submit for approval retroactively within 48 hours
- VP approval required

### 14.3 Bleisure Travel (Business + Leisure)

Personal days added to business trip:
- Allowed with manager approval
- Personal costs clearly separated
- Company pays business days only
- Hotel: Allocate nights proportionally

---

## 15. Policy Violations

### 15.1 Minor Violations

- Expense over limit by <10%: Warning
- Missing receipts: Expense rejected
- Late submission: Delay in reimbursement

### 15.2 Major Violations

- Fraudulent expenses: Termination
- Repeated policy violations: Performance review
- Excessive unauthorized expenses: Repayment + discipline

### 15.3 Monitoring

Finance reviews monthly:
- Top 10 expenses by amount
- Policy compliance rate
- Exception trends
