## 8. Data Storage and Security

### 8.1 Storage Locations

**Primary data center**: AWS Tokyo region (ap-northeast-1)  
**Backup data center**: AWS Osaka region (ap-northeast-3)  
**Data residency**: Customer data stays in Japan unless customer consents to international transfer

**For EU customers**:
- Data stored in AWS Frankfurt (eu-central-1) or customer's chosen EU region
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for any transfers

### 8.2 Security Measures

**Technical controls** (see Information Security Policy for details):
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Multi-factor authentication
- Access controls and least privilege
- Regular security testing
- Intrusion detection
- Backup and disaster recovery

**Organizational controls**:
- Security training for all staff
- Background checks for employees
- Confidentiality agreements
- Incident response procedures
- Regular security audits

### 8.3 Data Breach Response

**If breach occurs**:
1. Contain breach immediately (<2 hours)
2. Assess impact (what data, how many affected)
3. Notify regulators within 72 hours (APPI/GDPR requirement)
4. Notify affected individuals without undue delay
5. Public disclosure if material
6. Offer remediation (identity monitoring if appropriate)

**See**: Security Incident DC_YUKI_005 for precedent of transparent breach response.

## 9. Data Retention

### 9.1 Customer Data

**Active customers**:
- Account data: Duration of subscription
- Usage data: 90 days (rolling)
- Content data: Duration of subscription + 30 days after cancellation
- Support data: 2 years

**Inactive customers** (post-cancellation):
- Account data: 30 days (then deleted)
- Content data: 30 days (then deleted unless legal hold)
- Billing data: 7 years (tax/legal requirement)

**Marketing data**:
- Opted-in contacts: Until opt-out + 30 days
- Opted-out contacts: Suppression list (permanent, to honor opt-out)

### 9.2 Employee Data

**Current employees**:
- All employment data retained

**Former employees**:
- Personnel file: 7 years after termination
- Payroll records: 7 years (tax requirement)
- Performance reviews: 3 years
- Other data: Deleted within 1 year unless legal hold

### 9.3 Backups

**Backup retention**:
- Daily backups: 30 days
- Weekly backups: 90 days
- Quarterly backups: 1 year

**Backup deletion**: Overwritten after retention period, not individually deletable (technical limitation).

**Legal holds**: If data subject to litigation/investigation, retained until hold lifted.

## 10. Data Subject Rights

### 10.1 Your Rights (APPI & GDPR)

**Right to access**: Request copy of your personal data

**Right to rectification**: Correct inaccurate data

**Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten"): Delete data when no longer necessary

**Right to restriction**: Limit how we use data

**Right to portability**: Receive data in machine-readable format

**Right to object**: Object to certain processing (e.g., marketing)

**Right to withdraw consent**: For consent-based processing

**Right to complain**: Lodge complaint with supervisory authority

### 10.2 How to Exercise Rights

**Submit request**:
- **Email**: privacy@nexatech.jp
- **Web form**: https://nexatech.jp/privacy-request
- **Mail**: NexaTech Solutions, Privacy Team, [Address]

**Required information**:
- Your name and email (to verify identity)
- Account information (if applicable)
- Specific request (access, delete, etc.)
- Preferred response method

**Response timeline**:
- Acknowledge request: Within 72 hours
- Fulfill request: Within 30 days (APPI/GDPR requirement)
- Complex requests: May extend to 60 days with explanation

**Verification**:
- We verify identity before fulfilling requests
- May request additional information to confirm identity
- Prevents unauthorized access to your data

### 10.3 Self-Service Options

**In-product controls**:
- Update account information: Account Settings
- Download your data: Export feature
- Delete your account: Account Settings > Delete Account
- Manage email preferences: Unsubscribe links
- Manage cookies: Cookie preferences

## 18. Privacy by Design

### 18.1 Principles

**Data minimization**:
- Collect only necessary data
- Don't collect "just in case"
- Regularly review and delete unnecessary data

**Purpose limitation**:
- Use data only for stated purpose
- Don't repurpose without consent
- Be transparent about uses

**Storage limitation**:
- Retain only as long as necessary
- Automatic deletion policies
- Secure disposal

**Accuracy**:
- Keep data up-to-date
- Allow customer corrections
- Verify data quality

**Security**:
- Encryption and access controls
- Regular security testing
- Incident response procedures

**Accountability**:
- Document compliance
- Regular privacy impact assessments
- Train staff on privacy

### 18.2 Privacy Impact Assessments

**When required**:
- New products/features processing sensitive data
- New vendors with data access
- Changes to data processing purposes
- Automated decision-making systems

**Assessment includes**:
- What data is processed
- Legal basis for processing
- Risk assessment
- Mitigation measures
- Alternatives considered
