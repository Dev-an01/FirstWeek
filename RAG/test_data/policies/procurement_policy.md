## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for purchasing goods and services to ensure:
- Cost-effective procurement
- Proper vendor evaluation and selection
- Compliance with budget approvals
- Risk management in vendor relationships
- Ethical purchasing practices

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all purchases of:
- Software and SaaS subscriptions
- Hardware and equipment
- Professional services (consulting, legal, audit)
- Marketing and advertising services
- Office supplies and facilities
- Travel and events (see separate Travel Policy)

**Exclusions**: Employee compensation, benefits, and reimbursements (separate policies apply)

---

## 3. Approval Thresholds

### 3.1 Purchase Approval Limits

| Amount (¥) | Approval Required | Vendor Selection | RFP Required |
|-----------|------------------|------------------|--------------|
| < 500K | Requestor + Manager | Single vendor OK | No |
| 500K - 2M | Manager + Department Head | 2 quotes preferred | No |
| 2M - 10M | Department Head + CFO | 3 quotes required | Recommended |
| 10M - 30M | VP + CFO | 3 quotes required | Yes |
| > 30M | VP + CFO + CEO | Formal RFP process | Yes |

### 3.2 Contract Duration Considerations

**Multi-year contracts**:
- Total contract value used for approval (not annual)
- Example: ¥8M/year × 3 years = ¥24M (requires VP + CFO approval)

**Automatic renewals**:
- Treated as new contract at renewal
- Re-approval required if value exceeds thresholds

## 4. Vendor Selection Process

### 4.1 Pre-Qualified Vendors

**Preferred vendors** (pre-approved for recurring purchases):
- Office supplies: Office Depot KK
- Cloud infrastructure: AWS, Google Cloud
- Travel booking: JTB Business Travel
- Shipping: Yamato Transport

**Benefits**:
- Faster procurement (no vendor evaluation)
- Negotiated corporate rates
- Established payment terms

### 4.2 New Vendor Evaluation

**For purchases >¥2M**, evaluate vendors on:

**Capability** (40%):
- Experience and track record
- References from similar companies
- Technical expertise
- Capacity to deliver

**Cost** (30%):
- Total cost of ownership
- Payment terms
- Discount opportunities
- Hidden costs

**Risk** (20%):
- Financial stability
- Data security (if accessing our data)
- Business continuity plans
- Insurance coverage

**Cultural Fit** (10%):
- Values alignment
- Communication style
- Customer service approach
- Long-term partnership potential

### 4.3 Vendor Due Diligence

**For vendors with data access** (see Information Security Policy):
- ☐ Security questionnaire
- ☐ SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification
- ☐ Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- ☐ Insurance verification (cyber liability)
- ☐ References checked

**For all vendors >¥10M**:
- ☐ Financial statements reviewed
- ☐ Credit check
- ☐ Business registration verified
- ☐ Anti-corruption/sanctions screening

## 5. Request for Proposal (RFP) Process

### 5.1 When RFP Required

**Mandatory for**:
- Purchases >¥10M
- Strategic vendor relationships
- Complex projects with multiple vendors
- Government/regulated industry requirements

**Optional but recommended for**:
- Purchases ¥2M - ¥10M
- First-time vendor categories
- Competitive markets with many options

### 5.2 RFP Structure

**RFP must include**:
1. Company background and context
2. Project scope and requirements
3. Technical specifications
4. Timeline and milestones
5. Evaluation criteria and weights
6. Budget range (optional)
7. Proposal format and deadline
8. Terms and conditions

**Timeline**:
- RFP preparation: 1-2 weeks
- Vendor response period: 2-3 weeks
- Evaluation and selection: 1-2 weeks
- Negotiation: 1-2 weeks
- **Total**: 6-9 weeks

### 5.3 Vendor Shortlisting

**Minimum 3 vendors** invited to submit proposals

**Shortlist criteria**:
- Meet minimum requirements
- Appropriate company size/experience
- Geographic presence (if relevant)
- Budget compatibility

**Exceptions**: Sole-source procurement (Section 5.5)

### 5.4 Proposal Evaluation

**Evaluation committee**:
- Requestor (business owner)
- Department head
- Finance representative
- Technical expert (if applicable)

**Scoring**:
- Blind evaluation (remove vendor names initially)
- Each committee member scores independently
- Discuss discrepancies
- Consensus decision

**Documentation**:
- Evaluation matrix with scores
- Decision rationale
- Vendor feedback (if requested)

### 5.5 Sole-Source Justification

**When only one vendor acceptable**:
- Proprietary/patented solution
- Existing vendor lock-in (migration cost prohibitive)
- Emergency/time-critical situation
- Regulatory requirement

**Approval required**:
- Written justification
- CFO approval (all sole-source >¥2M)
- CEO approval (sole-source >¥10M)

## 6. Contract Management

### 6.1 Contract Requirements

**All contracts >¥500K must include**:
- ☐ Scope of work / deliverables
- ☐ Payment terms and schedule
- ☐ Timeline and milestones
- ☐ Acceptance criteria
- ☐ Warranties and representations
- ☐ Limitation of liability
- ☐ Termination clause
- ☐ Data security terms (if applicable)
- ☐ Confidentiality/NDA
- ☐ Dispute resolution process

**Legal review required**:
- All contracts >¥5M
- All multi-year contracts
- Any non-standard terms
- Master Service Agreements (MSAs)

### 6.2 Contract Approval Workflow

1. **Requestor** initiates contract in procurement system
2. **Department head** approves business terms
3. **Finance** reviews pricing and payment terms
4. **Legal** reviews contract language (if required)
5. **CFO** approves (if >¥2M)
6. **CEO** approves (if >¥30M)
7. **Signature** by authorized signatory

**Timeline target**: 5 business days for standard contracts

### 6.3 Contract Storage

**Central repository**: All contracts stored in ContractWorks system
- Searchable by vendor, date, value, owner
- Automatic renewal reminders (90 days advance)
- Expiration tracking
- Amendment tracking

**Access**:
- Contract owner: Full access
- Department head: Department contracts
- Finance/Legal: All contracts
- Executives: All contracts

## 7. Software and SaaS Procurement

### 7.1 Software Categories

**Approved software** (no additional approval needed):
- Microsoft 365
- Google Workspace
- Slack
- Zoom
- Standard development tools

**Requires approval**:
- New SaaS subscriptions
- Seat expansions >10%
- Upgrades to higher tier

### 7.2 SaaS Evaluation Criteria

**Must evaluate**:
- ✓ Business need (is it solving real problem?)
- ✓ Existing alternatives (do we already have this capability?)
- ✓ Total cost of ownership (licenses + implementation + training)
- ✓ Integration with existing tools
- ✓ Data security and privacy
- ✓ Vendor stability and roadmap
- ✓ User adoption likelihood

**Build vs Buy assessment**:
- For core capabilities, consider building
- See CTO decision framework (DC_YUKI_002)
- Generally prefer buying non-core tools

### 7.3 SaaS Contract Terms

**Negotiate for**:
- Annual (not monthly) billing with discount
- Multi-year commitments with price lock
- Flexible seat scaling (up and down)
- Data export capabilities
- Service level agreements (SLAs)
- Termination rights (avoid long lock-ins)

**Red flags**:
- Automatic renewal without opt-out
- Aggressive price escalation clauses
- No data portability
- Broad indemnification requirements
- Unclear data ownership

## 10. Payment Terms and Processes

### 10.1 Standard Payment Terms

**Preferred**: Net 30 days

**Negotiable**:
- Net 45 days (for large vendors)
- Net 15 days (for small vendors, if discount offered)
- Progress payments (for large projects)

**Not acceptable**:
- Payment in advance (except subscriptions)
- Net 60+ days (cash flow inefficient)
- Payment on delivery (for large amounts)

### 10.2 Payment Methods

**Preferred methods**:
1. Bank transfer (domestic)
2. International wire (for foreign vendors)
3. Corporate credit card (for <¥200K)
4. Check (rare, only if required)

**Prohibited**:
- Cash payments >¥100K
- Personal credit cards for company purchases (except with reimbursement)
- Cryptocurrency (except with CFO approval)

### 10.3 Invoice Processing

**Requirements for payment**:
- ☐ Valid invoice with required details
- ☐ Matches purchase order
- ☐ Goods/services received and accepted
- ☐ Proper approvals in system
- ☐ No outstanding issues or disputes

**Processing timeline**:
- Invoice received → Accounts Payable
- Verification: 3 business days
- Approval routing: 2 business days
- Payment: Per agreed terms (typically Net 30)

**Late payments**:
- Target: <1% of invoices paid late
- Penalties avoided through proper planning

## 11. Vendor Management

### 11.1 Vendor Onboarding

**New vendors must provide**:
- Business registration
- Bank details (for payment)
- Tax identification
- W-9/W-8 (if applicable)
- Insurance certificates (if required)
- Signed vendor agreement

**Vendor portal**: Self-service onboarding at vendors.nexatech.jp

### 11.2 Vendor Performance Reviews

**Frequency**:
- Critical vendors (>¥10M annually): Quarterly
- Major vendors (¥2M - ¥10M): Bi-annually
- Other vendors: Annually or as needed

**Evaluation criteria**:
- Quality of deliverables
- Timeliness / adherence to deadlines
- Communication and responsiveness
- Value for money
- Issue resolution
- Innovation and proactive suggestions

**Rating scale**: 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent)

**Actions based on ratings**:
- 4-5: Maintain or expand relationship
- 3: Improvement plan required
- 1-2: Replacement evaluation initiated

### 11.3 Vendor Relationship Management

**Executive sponsors** assigned to strategic vendors:
- Quarterly business reviews
- Roadmap discussions
- Issue escalation path
- Contract renewal planning

**Vendor appreciation**:
- Annual vendor awards
- Recognition for exceptional performance
- Referrals and testimonials (when appropriate)

## 12. Budget Management

### 12.1 Budget Allocation

**Annual budgeting**:
- Each department receives budget allocation
- Further divided by category (software, services, equipment)
- Tracked monthly against actual spend

**Budget owners**:
- Responsible for staying within budget
- Can reallocate within department (with approval)
- Cannot exceed total department budget without CFO approval

### 12.2 Budget Variance

**Acceptable variance**: ±5%

**If exceeding budget**:
- <10% over: Justify to department head
- 10-20% over: CFO approval required
- >20% over: CEO approval required

**Underspent budget**:
- Not automatically rolled over to next year
- Request carryover with justification

### 12.3 Emergency Purchases

**Unbudgeted urgent needs**:
- Document business justification
- Obtain verbal approval (confirm in writing)
- Process through normal approval workflow retroactively
- Limited to genuine emergencies

**Examples**:
- System outage requiring immediate fix
- Critical security patch/tool
- Customer emergency requiring resources

## 13. Ethical Procurement

### 13.1 Conflicts of Interest

**Prohibited**:
- ❌ Purchasing from family/friends without disclosure
- ❌ Accepting gifts >¥10K from vendors
- ❌ Personal financial interest in vendor
- ❌ Steering contracts to vendors for personal benefit

**Required disclosure**:
- Any relationship with vendor (family, financial, personal)
- Gifts received >¥5K
- Outside board positions with vendors

**Recusal**: If conflict exists, remove yourself from decision process

### 13.2 Gifts and Entertainment

**Acceptable**:
- ✓ Modest meals (<¥10K per person)
- ✓ Promotional items (<¥5K value)
- ✓ Conference/event attendance (if business relevant)

**Requires approval**:
- Gifts ¥5K - ¥10K: Manager approval
- Entertainment >¥10K: VP approval
- Travel (vendor-paid): CFO approval

**Prohibited**:
- ❌ Cash or cash equivalents
- ❌ Gifts >¥10K
- ❌ Personal services
- ❌ Anything creating obligation

### 13.3 Kickbacks and Bribes

**Zero tolerance**: Any kickback or bribe results in immediate termination and potential legal action

**Report suspicions**: ethics@nexatech.jp (anonymous hotline available)

## 14. Sustainable and Responsible Procurement

### 14.1 Sustainability Considerations

**Preference for vendors who**:
- Have environmental policies
- Minimize packaging and waste
- Offer carbon-neutral shipping
- Use renewable energy
- Have recycling programs

**Green purchasing**:
- Energy-efficient equipment (Energy Star rated)
- Recycled/recyclable materials
- Local vendors (reduce shipping emissions)

### 14.2 Diversity and Inclusion

**Commitment**: Proactively include diverse vendors in RFP processes
- Women-owned businesses
- Minority-owned businesses
- Small businesses
- Social enterprises

**Not quota-based**: Quality and value remain primary, but give equal consideration

### 14.3 Ethical Sourcing

**Avoid vendors**:
- Using child labor or forced labor
- Violating human rights
- Engaging in corruption
- Causing environmental damage

**Due diligence**: For major vendors, review CSR reports and certifications

## 16. Exceptions and Special Cases

### 16.1 Exception Request Process

**When to request exception**:
- Sole-source justification
- Budget overrun
- Expedited approval needed
- Non-standard terms required

**How to request**:
- Submit via procurement system
- Document business justification
- Provide risk assessment
- Suggest mitigation

**Approval**: CFO (or CEO if >¥30M)

### 16.2 Emergency Procurement

**Emergency defined**:
- System outage affecting customers
- Security vulnerability requiring immediate fix
- Legal/regulatory deadline
- Health and safety issue

**Process**:
1. Obtain verbal approval from manager + CFO
2. Proceed with purchase
3. Submit documentation within 24 hours
4. Retroactive approval in system

**Abuse**: Repeated "emergencies" indicate poor planning, subject to review

## 17. Vendor Offboarding

### 17.1 Contract Termination

**Notice requirements**:
- Review contract termination clause
- Provide required notice period
- Document termination reason
- Confirm termination in writing

**Final deliverables**:
- Close out any open items
- Final invoice settlement
- Data return or deletion
- Remove vendor access to systems

### 17.2 Knowledge Transfer

**If replacing vendor**:
- Document current state
- Transition plan to new vendor
- Handover period (if applicable)
- Lessons learned documentation

### 17.3 Exit Formalities

- Retrieve company property
- Terminate system access
- Final payment processed
- Update vendor status in system
- Store contract and correspondence

## 18. Audit and Compliance

### 18.1 Internal Audits

**Frequency**: Annual procurement audit

**Scope**:
- Approval compliance
- Vendor selection process
- Contract terms
- Payment accuracy
- Policy adherence

**Findings**: Reported to CFO and Audit Committee

### 18.2 External Audits

**Financial auditors** review procurement as part of annual audit:
- Internal controls
- Segregation of duties
- Authorization levels
- Vendor relationships

**Compliance**: SOC 2 includes vendor management controls

**Procurement records retained**:
- Contracts: Duration + 7 years

- Invoices: 7 years (tax requirement)
- RFP documentation: 3 years
- Vendor evaluations: Duration of relationship + 3 years
