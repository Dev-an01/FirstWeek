## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for employee expense reimbursement to ensure:
- Fair compensation for business expenses
- Timely reimbursement
- Proper documentation and compliance
- Prevention of fraud

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees who incur business expenses on behalf of NexaTech

---

## 3. General Principles

### 3.1 Business Purpose

**Reimbursable expenses** must be:
- ✓ Necessary for business operations
- ✓ Reasonable in amount
- ✓ Properly documented
- ✓ Approved in advance (when required)

**Not reimbursable**:
- ❌ Personal expenses
- ❌ Commuting to regular office
- ❌ Expenses covered by other policies (e.g., travel meals covered by per diem)

### 3.2 Employee Responsibility

**You are responsible for**:
- Spending company money as if it were your own
- Obtaining proper approvals
- Submitting expenses promptly
- Providing accurate documentation
- Following all policy guidelines

### 3.3 Company Commitment

**We commit to**:
- Reimburse legitimate expenses fully
- Process reimbursements within 15 business days
- Provide clear guidelines
- Fair and consistent application

## 4. Reimbursable Expense Categories

### 4.1 Business Travel

**Covered**: See Travel Expense Policy (POLICY-FIN-004) for details
- Transportation (air, rail, taxi, rental car)
- Accommodations
- Meals (per diem or actual)
- Ground transportation

**Not covered here** (separate policy applies)

### 4.2 Professional Development

**Covered**: See Professional Development Policy (POLICY-HR-003) for details
- Conferences and training
- Professional memberships
- Books and learning materials
- Certifications

**Annual budget limits apply**

### 4.3 Home Office Setup

**For remote employees** (one-time):
- Office furniture: Up to ¥100,000
- Equipment: Company provides (not reimbursed)
- Ergonomic accessories: ¥20,000

**See**: Remote Work Policy (POLICY-HR-001)

### 4.4 Home Office Monthly Stipend

**For remote employees** (recurring):
- ¥5,000/month automatically paid
- No receipts required
- Covers: Internet, electricity, phone

### 4.5 Mobile Phone

**If personal phone used for business**:
- ¥3,000/month stipend (no receipts)
- Or company-provided phone

**Not both**

### 4.6 Coworking Space

**If remote employee needs occasional workspace**:
- Up to ¥30,000/month
- Must be approved by manager
- Receipts required
- Day passes OK, monthly membership OK

### 4.7 Client Entertainment

**Business meals with clients/prospects**:
- Up to ¥15,000 per person (standard)
- Up to ¥25,000 per person (executive-level)
- Requires: Attendees, business purpose, receipts

**Approval**:
- <¥50,000 total: Manager
- ≥¥50,000 total: VP Sales or CMO

**See**: Sales and marketing guidelines

### 4.8 Team Meals

**Team building meals** (manager hosted):
- Up to ¥5,000 per person
- Quarterly or as appropriate
- Manager approval
- Entire team invited (no exclusions)

**Not reimbursable**: Regular lunch with just 1-2 colleagues (personal expense)

### 4.9 Office Supplies

**Small purchases** for home office:
- Pens, notebooks, sticky notes: Up to ¥5,000/month
- Larger items: Request through IT/Facilities

**Process**: Expense and attach receipt

### 4.10 Software and Subscriptions

**Business tools** (small):
- <¥10,000/year: Expense with manager approval
- ≥¥10,000/year: Submit procurement request

**Examples**:
- Grammarly (writing)
- Notion (personal productivity)
- ChatGPT Plus (work use)

### 4.11 Parking and Tolls

**When driving for business**:
- Parking fees at client offices: Fully reimbursed
- Toll charges: Fully reimbursed
- Parking at home/office: Not reimbursed (personal choice to drive)

### 4.12 Local Transportation

**Business meetings** (not commuting):
- Taxi: Reasonable when necessary
- Train/bus: Fully reimbursed
- IC card (Suica/Pasmo): Can expense business trips

**Receipts**: Required for amounts >¥1,000

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### 5.1 Explicitly Not Covered

- ❌ Commuting to regular office location
- ❌ Personal meals (unless business travel)
- ❌ Personal entertainment
- ❌ Traffic fines and parking tickets
- ❌ Personal grooming (haircuts, spa, gym)
- ❌ Personal clothing (unless specific uniform required)
- ❌ Childcare and family care
- ❌ Lost or stolen personal items
- ❌ Personal legal fees
- ❌ Pet care
- ❌ Personal health and wellness (covered by benefits)

### 5.2 Gray Areas (Ask Before Expensing)

**Potentially reimbursable with justification**:
- Business attire for client-facing role
- Ergonomic equipment beyond standard
- Premium software tools
- Large client gifts

**Process**: Request pre-approval from manager + finance

---

## 6. Documentation Requirements

### 6.1 Required Information

**Every expense must include**:
- ☐ Itemized receipt (photo acceptable)
- ☐ Business purpose description
- ☐ Date of expense
- ☐ Amount (with currency if foreign)
- ☐ Category (from approved list)
- ☐ Attendees (for meals/entertainment)

### 6.2 Receipt Requirements

**Acceptable receipts**:
- ✓ Itemized receipt showing detail
- ✓ Photo of paper receipt
- ✓ PDF email receipt
- ✓ Digital receipt (from app)

**Not acceptable**:
- ❌ Credit card statement only (not itemized)
- ❌ Handwritten notes
- ❌ Receipts with faded ink (make copy immediately!)

### 6.3 Lost Receipt Procedure

**If receipt genuinely lost**:
1. Submit expense with explanation
2. Manager approval required
3. Finance may request additional verification
4. Limit: 3 lost receipts per year (pattern indicates carelessness)

### 6.4 Foreign Currency

**For international expenses**:
- Include original currency amount
- Conversion rate (use company credit card rate, or xe.com)
- Screenshot of exchange rate
- Will reimburse in JPY equivalent

## 7. Expense Submission Process

### 7.1 Submission Timeline

**Submit within 30 days** of expense

**Late submissions**:
- 30-60 days: Warning
- 60-90 days: Requires VP approval
- >90 days: Not reimbursed (policy violation)

**Rationale**: Timely submission for accurate accounting and tax

### 7.2 Submission Method

**Via expense system** (Expensify):
1. Log into Expensify
2. Upload receipt (photo or PDF)
3. Select category
4. Enter amount and description
5. Submit report
6. Route to manager for approval

**Mobile app**: Available for iOS and Android (preferred method)

### 7.3 Manager Approval

**Manager reviews**:
- Business purpose appropriate
- Amount reasonable
- Receipt provided
- Policy compliant

**Approval timeline**: 3 business days

**If denied**: Manager explains reason, employee can revise or appeal

### 7.4 Finance Review

**Finance checks**:
- Manager approved
- Receipts complete
- Accounting codes correct
- Policy compliant
- No fraud indicators

**Approval timeline**: 2 business days

---

## 8. Reimbursement Payment

### 8.1 Payment Method

**Reimbursement via**:
- Bank transfer to registered account
- Or payroll (next pay period)

**Employee chooses** method in expense system

### 8.2 Payment Timeline

**Standard**: 15 business days from submission

**Breakdown**:
- Manager approval: 3 days
- Finance review: 2 days
- Payment processing: 10 days

**Delays**: If additional information needed, timeline resets

### 8.3 Payment Confirmation

**Notification** when:
- Expense approved
- Payment processed
- Funds transferred

**Check**: Bank account or next paycheck

- Employees with frequent expenses (>¥50K/month)

**Responsibilities**:

- Business travel
- Client entertainment

- Business supplies/equipment

**Prohibited uses**:

- Cash advances

## 10. Audit and Compliance

### 10.1 Expense Audits

**Finance randomly audits**:
- 5% of expense reports monthly
- 100% of expenses >¥50,000
- Pattern analysis (unusual spending)

**Audit includes**:
- Receipt verification
- Business purpose validity
- Policy compliance
- Fraud detection

### 10.2 Tax Compliance

**For company**:
- Expenses properly categorized for tax
- Receipts retained 7 years
- Entertainment expenses tracked (tax limits)

**For employee**:
- Reimbursements are tax-free (business expenses)
- Taxable benefits reported (e.g., personal use of company car)

### 10.3 Record Retention

**Employee**: Keep receipts until reimbursed (then can discard)

**Company**: Retain all expense documentation for 7 years (tax law)

---

## 11. Fraud Prevention

### 11.1 Fraud Examples

**Expense fraud includes**:
- Submitting personal expenses as business
- Inflating amounts
- Duplicate submissions
- Fabricating receipts
- Claiming expenses never incurred
- Splitting expenses to avoid approval limits

### 11.2 Detection

**Finance monitors for**:
- Duplicate expenses
- Round numbers (suspicious pattern)
- Expenses without receipts
- Unusual patterns (sudden spike)
- Same vendor repeatedly
- Weekend/holiday expenses (without travel)

### 11.3 Consequences

**Fraud results in**:
- Immediate termination
- Repayment of fraudulent amounts
- Legal action (prosecution for theft)
- Report to authorities

**No tolerance**: Even small fraud is termination offense

## 12. Exceptions and Appeals

### 12.1 Requesting Exception

**If policy doesn't cover legitimate business expense**:
1. Discuss with manager before incurring
2. Submit written justification
3. CFO approval required for exceptions
4. Document for future policy update

**Recent exceptions** (now policy):
- Coworking space stipend (added to policy)
- ChatGPT Plus subscription (now allowed)

### 12.2 Appealing Denial

**If expense denied**:
1. Understand denial reason
2. Provide additional information if applicable
3. Appeal to manager's manager or finance
4. CFO has final decision

**Most appeals result from**:
- Missing receipts (can be remedied)
- Unclear business purpose (clarify)
- Amount excessive (partial reimbursement possible)

## 13. Special Situations

### 13.1 Relocation Expenses

**If relocating for work** (company-requested):
- Moving costs: Up to ¥500,000
- Temporary housing: Up to 30 days
- Travel for apartment hunting: Covered

**Requires**: Pre-approval from HR and finance

**Not covered**: Personal choice relocations

### 13.2 Emergency Expenses

**Genuine emergencies** (customer crisis, system outage):
- Expense first, approve later
- Document emergency nature
- Submit within 3 days
- Retroactive approval

**Abuse**: "Emergency" more than once per quarter is suspicious

### 13.3 Shared Expenses

**If paying for colleagues**:
- OK for business meals/activities
- Clearly list all attendees
- Each person's amount
- Business purpose

**Colleague reimbursement**: Their expense, they submit separately (don't double-claim)

## 14. International Employees

### 14.1 Currency Differences

**Expense in foreign currency**:
- Receipt in original currency
- Conversion rate documented
- Reimbursed in employee's local currency (or JPY)

### 14.2 Tax Treatment

**May vary by country**:
- Some benefits taxable in one country, not another
- HR coordinates with tax advisors
- Employee receives proper tax forms

### 14.3 Compliance

**Must comply with**:
- Company policy (this document)
- Local tax and labor law
- If conflict, more generous provision applies
