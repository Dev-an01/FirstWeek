## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes behavioral and ethical standards for everyone at NexaTech to:
- Create respectful, inclusive workplace
- Uphold company values
- Comply with laws and regulations
- Protect company reputation
- Guide ethical decision-making

## 4. Professional Conduct

### 4.1 Respect and Dignity

**We commit to**:
- ✓ Treat everyone with respect regardless of position
- ✓ Listen actively and consider diverse perspectives
- ✓ Assume positive intent
- ✓ Give constructive feedback professionally
- ✓ Celebrate others' successes

**We do NOT tolerate**:
- ❌ Harassment or discrimination (see Section 5)
- ❌ Bullying or intimidation
- ❌ Public humiliation or shaming
- ❌ Retaliation against those who speak up
- ❌ Gossip or undermining colleagues

### 4.2 Professionalism

**Expected behaviors**:
- Be punctual for meetings
- Respond to communications timely
- Meet commitments and deadlines
- Dress appropriately for context (casual office, business for clients)
- Maintain appropriate boundaries

**Unacceptable behaviors**:
- Chronic lateness without communication
- Ghosting colleagues or customers
- Inappropriate language or jokes
- Unprofessional social media posts about company

### 4.3 Communication Standards

**In all communication**:
- Be clear and concise
- Be respectful in tone
- Avoid ALL CAPS (shouting)
- Use inclusive language
- Consider cultural differences
- Disagree constructively

**Email/Slack etiquette**:
- Reply within 24 hours (business days)
- Use "Reply All" judiciously
- No sensitive topics in group channels
- Respect working hours (use scheduled send)

## 5. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

### 5.1 Zero Tolerance Policy

NexaTech has **zero tolerance** for:
- Harassment (sexual, verbal, physical, visual)
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Retaliation against those who report

**Consequences**: Immediate investigation, up to and including termination

### 5.2 Protected Characteristics

**We do NOT discriminate based on**:
- Race, color, ethnicity, national origin
- Gender, gender identity, gender expression
- Sexual orientation
- Age
- Religion or belief
- Disability (physical or mental)
- Marital or family status
- Pregnancy or parental status
- Political affiliation
- Any other characteristic protected by law

### 5.3 Sexual Harassment

**Prohibited behaviors**:
- Unwelcome sexual advances or propositions
- Requests for sexual favors
- Sexual comments, jokes, or innuendo
- Sharing sexual images or content
- Unwanted physical contact
- Creating hostile work environment

**Applies to**:
- Supervisor-employee relationships
- Peer-to-peer interactions
- Vendor/customer interactions
- Online and in-person

### 5.4 Other Forms of Harassment

**Also prohibited**:
- Verbal harassment (insults, slurs, threats)
- Physical harassment (unwanted touching, blocking)
- Visual harassment (offensive images, gestures)
- Cyberbullying (online harassment)
- Microaggressions (subtle discrimination)

### 5.5 Romantic Relationships

**Supervisor-subordinate relationships**: Prohibited due to power dynamics

**Peer relationships**: Allowed but must be disclosed to HR
- Maintain professionalism at work
- No favoritism
- If relationship ends, maintain professionalism

**Client relationships**: Strongly discouraged (conflicts of interest)

## 7. Conflicts of Interest

### 7.1 Definition

**Conflict of interest**: When personal interests could improperly influence business decisions

### 7.2 Common Examples

**Must disclose**:
- Financial interest in vendor/customer/competitor
- Family member works at vendor/customer/competitor
- Outside employment or board positions
- Personal relationship with vendor contact
- Receiving gifts >¥10,000 from vendor

### 7.3 Disclosure Process

**How to disclose**:
1. Email HR and your manager
2. Describe the potential conflict
3. Proposed mitigation (e.g., recuse from decision)
4. HR documents and advises

**Not penalty**: Disclosure is responsible, concealment is violation

### 7.4 Outside Activities

**Allowed with disclosure**:
- Side businesses (not competing with NexaTech)
- Board positions (not competing)
- Consulting work (limited hours, no IP conflicts)

**Requires approval**:
- Any outside work >10 hours/week
- Any work in related industry
- Any use of NexaTech resources

**Prohibited**:
- Competing directly with NexaTech
- Using company time for outside work
- Using confidential info for personal gain

## 8. Confidentiality and Information Security

### 8.1 Confidential Information

**Must protect**:
- Customer data (names, usage, content)
- Employee data (salaries, reviews, personal info)
- Company financials and strategy
- Product roadmaps and IP
- Trade secrets and proprietary methods

**See**: Information Security Policy (POLICY-IT-001) for full requirements

### 8.2 Social Media

**Personal social media**:
- ✓ Can share public company news
- ✓ Can express personal opinions (with disclaimer)
- ✓ Be respectful and professional

**Prohibited**:
- ❌ Sharing confidential information
- ❌ Speaking on behalf of company (unless authorized)
- ❌ Disparaging company, colleagues, customers
- ❌ Harassment or discrimination online

**Guideline**: Would you say it in front of your CEO? If not, don't post it.

### 8.3 Media and Public Speaking

**Media inquiries**:
- Refer to CMO or CEO
- Don't speak on company's behalf unless authorized

**Public speaking** about company:
- Get approval from manager + CMO
- Use approved messaging
- Make clear if personal views

## 9. Protecting Company Assets

### 9.1 Physical Assets

**Proper use of**:
- Company equipment (laptops, phones, etc.)
- Office facilities and resources
- Vehicles (if applicable)

**Prohibited**:
- Theft or misuse of company property
- Excessive personal use
- Lending to non-employees
- Unauthorized removal from premises

### 9.2 Intellectual Property

**Company owns**:
- Work product created during employment
- Inventions related to company business
- Code, designs, documents created for work

**Employee owns**:
- Personal projects (unrelated to company)
- Work done entirely on own time with own resources
- Pre-existing IP brought to company

**Gray areas**: Discuss with manager and legal

### 9.3 Time and Productivity

**Expected**:
- Work your scheduled hours
- Meet commitments and deadlines
- Be present and engaged

**Acceptable**:
- Reasonable personal use of work time (quick personal call, etc.)
- Breaks and rest periods
- Flexible schedule (with manager agreement)

**Unacceptable**:
- Chronic underperformance
- Extended personal activities during work time
- Falsifying timesheets
- Working for another employer during company time

## 11. Health, Safety, and Wellness

### 11.1 Safe Workplace

**Company provides**:
- Safe working environment
- Ergonomic equipment
- Emergency procedures
- First aid supplies

**Employee responsibility**:
- Follow safety procedures
- Report hazards immediately
- Use equipment properly
- Participate in safety training

### 11.2 Substance Use

**Prohibited at work**:
- Illegal drugs (zero tolerance)
- Alcohol during work hours (except approved company events)
- Being under influence at work
- Possessing illegal substances on premises

**Company events with alcohol**:
- Drink responsibly
- Don't pressure others to drink
- Arrange safe transportation

**Support available**: If you have substance abuse issue, contact HR confidentially for assistance

### 11.3 Workplace Violence

**Zero tolerance** for:
- Physical violence or threats
- Weapons on premises (except authorized security)
- Intimidating or threatening behavior
- Domestic violence spilling into workplace

**If you feel unsafe**: Contact HR, your manager, or security immediately

**Emergency**: Call police (110 in Japan)

### 11.4 Mental Health

**We support mental health** through:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) - confidential counseling
- Mental health days (sick leave)
- Flexible work arrangements
- Supportive culture (no stigma)

**Resources**: Contact HR for EAP information

## 14. Reporting Violations

### 14.1 Duty to Report

**All employees must report**:
- Code of Conduct violations
- Illegal activity
- Safety hazards
- Harassment or discrimination
- Financial irregularities
- Conflicts of interest

**"If you see something, say something"**

### 14.2 How to Report

**Multiple channels**:
1. **Your manager** (first option for most issues)
2. **HR team**: people@nexatech.jp
3. **Ethics hotline**: ethics@nexatech.jp
4. **Anonymous hotline**: 0120-XXX-XXXX (24/7, third-party)
5. **CEO or any executive** (open door policy)

**For security issues**: security-incident@nexatech.jp

**For harassment/discrimination**: Can skip manager, go directly to HR

### 14.3 Whistleblower Protection

**No retaliation** for good-faith reporting:
- Protected from termination
- Protected from demotion or pay reduction
- Protected from harassment
- Confidentiality maintained (as much as possible)

**Retaliation is separate violation**: Person retaliating will be disciplined

**Bad faith reporting**: False allegations made with malicious intent are also violations

## 15. Investigation Process

### 15.1 How Investigations Work

**When violation reported**:
1. HR receives report (24-hour acknowledgment)
2. Initial assessment (severity and urgency)
3. Investigation begins (interviews, document review)
4. Interim measures if needed (separation of parties)
5. Findings and conclusions
6. Disciplinary action if warranted
7. Follow-up and monitoring

**Timeline**: Most investigations complete within 30 days

### 15.2 Cooperation

**All employees must**:
- Participate honestly in investigations
- Provide requested documents
- Maintain confidentiality
- Not interfere or retaliate

**Refusal to cooperate**: Itself a violation

### 15.3 Confidentiality

**Investigations are confidential**:
- Shared only with those who need to know
- Results not publicly disclosed
- Protects all parties involved

**Not completely secret**: May need to share with legal, regulators, or law enforcement

## 16. Consequences of Violations

### 16.1 Range of Discipline

**Depending on severity**:
- **Verbal warning**: Minor first offense
- **Written warning**: Documented in file
- **Performance improvement plan**: Specific violations to correct
- **Suspension**: Serious violations, pending investigation
- **Termination**: Major violations or repeated offenses
- **Legal action**: Fraud, theft, illegal activity

### 16.2 Factors Considered

**In determining discipline**:
- Severity of violation
- Intent (deliberate vs. mistake)
- Prior disciplinary history
- Impact on company/colleagues
- Cooperation during investigation
- Position of responsibility

### 16.3 Immediate Termination Offenses

**Violations resulting in immediate termination**:
- Violence or threats
- Theft or embezzlement
- Fraud (expense, financial reporting, etc.)
- Serious harassment or discrimination
- Breach of confidentiality (intentional)
- Gross insubordination
- Falsifying records
- Competing with company
- Any illegal activity

## 17. Manager Responsibilities

### 17.1 Leading by Example

**Managers must**:
- Model ethical behavior
- Set tone for their team
- Enforce Code of Conduct consistently
- Create safe environment for reporting

**Held to higher standard**: Leadership position requires it

### 17.2 Preventing Violations

**Proactive steps**:
- Discuss Code in team meetings
- Address concerns early (before they escalate)
- Recognize and reward ethical behavior
- Make expectations clear

### 17.3 Responding to Issues

**When team member reports concern**:
- Listen without judgment
- Thank them for reporting
- Take it seriously (don't dismiss)
- Report to HR immediately
- Don't investigate yourself (unless qualified)
- Follow up appropriately

**Never retaliate** or allow retaliation

## 22. Questions and Guidance

**Need guidance on ethical decision?** Ask yourself:

1. **Is it legal?**
2. **Is it aligned with our values?**
3. **Would I be comfortable if this were public?**
4. **How would it look to customers? To my family?**
5. **Am I treating others how I want to be treated?**

**If unsure**: Ask your manager, HR, or Legal before acting

**"When in doubt, reach out"**
