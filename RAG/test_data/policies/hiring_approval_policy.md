## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes hiring approval process to ensure:
- Hiring aligns with business needs and budget
- Proper cost planning and financial controls
- Efficient approval workflow
- Headcount management

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all new hires:
- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees
- Contract/temporary workers (>3 months)
- Interns

Does NOT apply to:
- Replacements for budgeted roles (see Section 4.2)
- Short-term contractors (<3 months, within budget)

---

## 3. Approval Authority

### 3.1 Approval Thresholds

| Role Level | Annual Comp | Approval Required |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Individual Contributor | <¥10M | Hiring Manager + Department Head |
| Senior IC / Manager | ¥10M - ¥15M | Department Head + VP + CFO |
| Director | ¥15M - ¥20M | VP + CFO + CEO |
| VP / C-Level | >¥20M | CEO + Board Compensation Committee |

**Note**: "Annual Comp" = Base + Target Bonus/Commission + Benefits (~30% loaded)

## 4. Hiring Request Process

### 4.1 New Position (Not in Budget)

**Process**:
1. **Business case required** (template in HR system)
   - Why is role needed?
   - What business impact?
   - What happens if we don't hire?
   - ROI calculation (if applicable)
   - Budget impact

2. **Department head approval**
   - Validates business need
   - Confirms priority vs. other roles
   - Allocates from department budget

3. **CFO review**
   - Financial impact assessment
   - Headcount budget impact
   - Compensation benchmarking
   - Approves if justified

4. **CEO approval** (if >¥15M or strategic)
   - Strategic alignment
   - Company priorities
   - Final decision

**Timeline**: 5-10 business days

**Context**: See DC_RAJ_001 for precedent (Sales team expansion request)

### 4.2 Replacement Hire (Budgeted)

**Process** (simplified):
1. **Hiring manager submits** replacement request
2. **HR verifies** position was budgeted
3. **Department head approves** (no CFO needed if like-for-like)
4. **Post immediately**

**Timeline**: 1-2 business days

**Definition**: "Like-for-like" = same level, same compensation range

**If upgrade**: Treat as new position (requires CFO approval)

### 4.3 Backfill After Departure

**Automatic approval** if:
- Departure occurred <90 days ago
- Position was budgeted
- Hiring at same or lower level

**Delayed backfill** (>90 days):
- Re-justify business need
- Market may have changed
- Department head approval required

**Not automatic**: Company may decide not to backfill (re-org, changed priorities)

### 4.4 Emergency Hiring

**Definition**: Critical business need requiring urgent hire
- Key customer escalation
- System outage requiring specialized skill
- Legal/compliance requirement

**Process**:
- Verbal approval from VP + CFO
- Written justification within 24 hours
- Retroactive formal approval

**Limit**: 2 emergency hires per department per year

## 5. Job Description and Leveling

### 5.1 Job Description Required

**Before posting, JD must include**:
- Role title and level
- Department and reporting structure
- Key responsibilities (5-8 bullets)
- Required qualifications
- Preferred qualifications
- Compensation range
- Location (office/remote)

**Template**: Available in HR system

**Review**: HR reviews for:
- Appropriate leveling
- Competitive requirements
- Legal compliance (no discriminatory language)
- Inclusive language

### 5.2 Leveling Standards

**Engineering example**:
- Junior Engineer (Level 1): ¥5M - ¥7M
- Engineer (Level 2): ¥7M - ¥10M
- Senior Engineer (Level 3): ¥10M - ¥14M
- Staff Engineer (Level 4): ¥14M - ¥18M
- Principal Engineer (Level 5): ¥18M - ¥24M

**Leveling guidelines**: See Career Frameworks (Professional Development Policy)

**Consistency**: HR ensures equivalent levels across departments

## 6. Compensation Approval

### 6.1 Compensation Bands

**Each role has defined band**:
- Minimum: Entry-level for that role
- Midpoint: Experienced performer
- Maximum: Expert/top performer

**Example - Senior Engineer**:
- Min: ¥10M
- Mid: ¥12M
- Max: ¥14M

**New hire typically**: 10th-50th percentile (room for growth)

### 6.2 Exceeding Band

**If candidate requires above-band compensation**:
- Justify in writing (unique skills, competitive offer, market conditions)
- CFO approval required
- Consider: Level up instead (if qualifications match)

**Red flag**: Consistently hiring above band indicates band needs adjustment

### 6.3 Equity/Stock Options

**For employees eligible for equity**:
- Grant size based on level and role
- Approved by CEO
- Board approval for VP+ grants
- Subject to equity plan limits

**Current practice**: Directors and above receive equity

## 7. Budget Impact Assessment

### 7.1 Fully Loaded Cost

**CFO calculates**:
- Base salary
- Bonuses/commission (target)
- Benefits (~30% of base in Japan)
  - Health insurance
  - Pension
  - Unemployment insurance
  - Other statutory benefits
- Equipment and onboarding (~¥500K one-time)
- Workspace costs (if office-based)

**Example**:
- Senior Engineer base: ¥12M
- Benefits: ¥3.6M (30%)
- Equipment: ¥500K (one-time)
- **Total Year 1**: ¥16.1M
- **Ongoing annual**: ¥15.6M

### 7.2 Budget Allocation

**Hiring reduces available budget**:
- Department budget: ¥450M for Engineering
- Hire Senior Engineer: ¥15.6M annual
- Remaining budget: ¥434.4M

**Tracking**: Finance tracks in real-time dashboard

### 7.3 Budget Overruns

**If department exceeds budget**:
- Must justify to CFO
- May require offsetting (defer other hire, reduce spend elsewhere)
- CEO approval for >10% overage

## 8. Hiring Timeline

### 8.1 Standard Timeline

**Typical timeline** (from approval to start):

| Phase | Duration | Responsible |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Job posting preparation | 3-5 days | Hiring Manager + HR |
| Active sourcing | 2-4 weeks | Recruiter + Hiring Manager |
| Interviews | 2-3 weeks | Interview panel |
| Reference checks | 3-5 days | HR |
| Offer preparation | 2-3 days | HR + CFO (if needed) |
| Candidate decision | 3-7 days | Candidate |
| Notice period | 4-8 weeks (Japan standard) | Candidate |
| **Total** | **10-16 weeks** | |

**Expedited hiring**: Possible for critical roles (with VP approval)

### 8.2 Time to Fill Targets

**By role type**:
- Junior roles: 6-8 weeks
- Mid-level roles: 8-12 weeks
- Senior roles: 12-16 weeks
- Executive roles: 16-24 weeks

**Measured**: Time from approval to start date

## 9. Internal vs. External Hiring

### 9.1 Internal Candidates First

**Policy**: Post internally for 5 days before external posting

**Benefits**:
- Lower cost (no recruiter fees)
- Faster onboarding
- Known performer
- Employee development and retention

**Process**: Internal candidates apply via HR system

### 9.2 Internal Hiring Approval

**Simplified process**:
- Hiring manager approval
- Current manager notified (transparency)
- HR facilitates transition
- 4-week transition period (typically)

**Budget**: No additional headcount (transfer)

---

## 10. Contractor and Temporary Workers

### 10.1 When to Use Contractors

**Appropriate for**:
- Short-term projects (<6 months)
- Specialized skills not needed long-term
- Seasonal/fluctuating workload
- Trial before full-time hire

**Not appropriate for**:
- Core long-term work
- Avoiding headcount limits
- Replacing full-time employees

### 10.2 Contractor Approval

**Process**:
- Statement of work (SOW) prepared
- Manager approval
- If >3 months or >¥5M: CFO approval
- Follows procurement policy for vendor selection

**Budget**: Comes from operating budget, not headcount budget

### 10.3 Contractor to Full-Time

**If converting contractor to employee**:
- Follows standard hiring approval
- Counts toward headcount
- Simplified interview process (already working together)
- Fair to other candidates (post if required)

---

## 11. Internship Programs

### 11.1 Intern Hiring

**Annual intern program**:
- Summer interns (June-August)
- Budget allocated annually
- Hiring manager proposes project
- HR coordinates program

**Approval**:
- Department head approval
- CFO approves total intern budget
- Individual intern hiring: Manager decision

### 11.2 Intern to Full-Time

**If offering full-time role**:
- Follows standard approval process
- Strong performance as intern is factor
- Still must meet hiring bar (interview)
- Counts toward headcount budget

## 12. Special Hiring Situations

### 12.1 Rehires (Boomerang Employees)

**Former employees returning**:
- HR reviews departure circumstances
- Interview process may be abbreviated
- Compensation: Current market rate (not last salary)
- Manager and HR approval required

**Ineligible for rehire**: Terminated for cause

### 12.2 Executive Hires

**VP and C-Level**:
- CEO-led search (often with executive recruiter)
- Board approval required
- Compensation committee approves package
- Extensive reference checks
- May include onboarding coach

### 12.3 Acquihires

**If acquiring company for talent**:
- CEO + CFO + Board approval
- Special compensation packages possible
- Integration plan required
- HR coordinates onboarding

## 13. Diversity and Inclusion in Hiring

### 13.1 Commitment

**NexaTech commits to**:
- Diverse candidate pipeline
- Unbiased hiring process
- Equal opportunity employment

**Targets** (aspirational, not quotas):
- 40%+ women in technical roles (currently 28%)
- Diverse interview panels
- Inclusive job descriptions

### 13.2 Hiring Process Requirements

**To ensure fairness**:
- Structured interviews (same questions for all)
- Diverse interview panel
- Blind resume review (when possible)
- Unconscious bias training for interviewers

## 14. Offer Approval and Extension

### 14.1 Offer Letter Preparation

**Includes**:
- Position title and level
- Reporting relationship
- Compensation (base, bonus, equity if applicable)
- Start date
- Benefits summary
- Contingencies (background check, etc.)

**Prepared by**: HR

**Approved by**: Hiring Manager + CFO (if new role)

### 14.2 Offer Negotiation

**Within band**: Hiring manager can negotiate

**Outside band or special terms**: CFO approval required

**Multiple rounds**: More than 2 counter-offers require escalation

### 14.3 Offer Acceptance

**Candidate signs**: Offer letter + onboarding paperwork

**Background check**: HR coordinates (after offer acceptance)

**Reneging**: Rare but possible; maintain professionalism

---

## 15. Monitoring and Reporting

### 15.1 Headcount Dashboard

**Real-time tracking**:
- Current headcount by department
- Approved but unfilled roles
- In-progress hires (by stage)
- Projected year-end headcount

**Access**: Department heads, Finance, HR, CEO

### 15.2 Hiring Metrics

**Tracked monthly**:
- Time to fill (by role type)
- Offer acceptance rate
- Cost per hire
- Hiring source effectiveness
- Diversity metrics
- Quality of hire (90-day retention)

**Reported**: Quarterly to executive team

### 15.3 Budget Variance

**Finance tracks**:
- Actual headcount vs. budget
- Actual compensation vs. budget
- Open position impact on budget

**Alert**: If >10% variance, escalate to CFO + CEO
