## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes sales compensation structure to:
- Align sales incentives with company revenue goals
- Reward high performance
- Promote sustainable customer acquisition
- Balance new business and renewals
- Ensure fair and transparent compensation

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to:
- Account Executives (AEs)
- Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)
- Account Managers (AMs)
- Sales management (VP Sales, Sales Directors)

Does NOT apply to:
- Customer Success (separate compensation plan)
- Pre-sales engineers (bonus based on company performance)
- Marketing (separate bonus structure)

---

## 3. Compensation Structure

### 3.1 OTE (On-Target Earnings)

**OTE = Base Salary + Target Commission**

Assumes 100% quota attainment

| Role | Base Salary | Target Commission | OTE | Variable % |
|------|-------------|-------------------|-----|------------|
| SDR | ¥4,500K | ¥1,500K | ¥6,000K | 25% |
| AE (Mid-Market) | ¥7,000K | ¥5,000K | ¥12,000K | 42% |
| AE (Enterprise) | ¥8,000K | ¥7,000K | ¥15,000K | 47% |
| Account Manager | ¥6,500K | ¥3,500K | ¥10,000K | 35% |
| Sales Director | ¥10,000K | ¥4,000K | ¥14,000K | 29% |
| VP Sales | ¥13,000K | ¥5,000K | ¥18,000K | 28% |

**Philosophy**: Higher variable % for individual contributors, lower for management

### 3.2 Base Salary

**Paid**: Monthly (12 equal installments)

**Adjustments**: Annual review based on performance and market

**Guaranteed**: Base salary is guaranteed regardless of sales performance

### 3.3 Commission

**Earned**: Based on closed deals and quota attainment

**Paid**: Monthly (for deals closed in previous month)

**Clawback**: See Section 8 for clawback conditions

## 4. Quota Setting

### 4.1 Quota Assignment

**Annual quotas** set by:
- Company revenue target
- Territory/segment allocation
- Historical performance
- Market opportunity
- Ramp time (for new hires)

**Example**: If company target is ¥4.5B revenue, and we have 10 AEs, average quota is ¥450M per AE (adjusted by territory)

### 4.2 Quota by Role

| Role | Annual Quota | Quota Type |
|------|--------------|------------|
| SDR | 120 SRLs (Sales-Ready Leads) | Activity-based |
| AE (Mid-Market) | ¥60M ARR | Revenue-based |
| AE (Enterprise) | ¥80M ARR | Revenue-based |
| Account Manager | ¥50M net retention + ¥15M upsell | Revenue-based |
| Sales Director | Team quota (sum of AEs) | Team revenue |
| VP Sales | Company new business revenue | Company revenue |

**Quarterly breakdown**: 
- Q1: 20% of annual (ramp-up)
- Q2: 25% of annual
- Q3: 25% of annual
- Q4: 30% of annual (strongest quarter)

### 4.3 Quota Adjustments

**Mid-year adjustments** allowed for:
- Territory changes
- Product launches/sunsets
- Market conditions (e.g., recession)
- Extended leave (>30 days)

**Process**:
- Sales rep or manager requests adjustment
- VP Sales reviews and approves
- CFO approves if significant (>20% change)

**Not adjusted for**:
- Individual performance issues
- Lost deals
- Competitive pressure (this is normal)

## 5. Commission Calculation

### 5.1 For Account Executives (New Business)

**Commission rate**: Tiered based on quota attainment

| Quota Attainment | Commission Rate | Example (¥80M quota) |
|-----------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 0-50% | 5% of ARR | ¥40M closed = ¥2,000K |
| 51-80% | 7% of ARR | ¥64M closed = ¥4,480K |
| 81-100% | 10% of ARR | ¥80M closed = ¥8,000K (hits OTE) |
| 101-125% | 12% of ARR | ¥100M closed = ¥12,000K |
| 126%+ | 15% of ARR | ¥120M closed = ¥18,000K (accelerator!) |

**Why tiered?**: Reward over-achievement ("accelerators")

**Example calculation** (AE with ¥80M quota):
- Closes ¥90M (113% of quota)
- 0-50% tier: ¥40M × 5% = ¥2,000K
- 51-80%: ¥24M × 7% = ¥1,680K
- 81-100%: ¥16M × 10% = ¥1,600K
- 101-113%: ¥10M × 12% = ¥1,200K
- **Total commission**: ¥6,480K

### 5.2 For SDRs (Lead Generation)

**Commission per SRL** (Sales-Ready Lead):

| SRL Quality | Commission | Criteria |
|-------------|------------|----------|
| Accepted SRL | ¥10K | Sales accepts lead within 24 hours |
| Qualified SRL | ¥15K | Converts to opportunity (BANT qualified) |
| Closed Deal | ¥50K | SRL results in closed deal (within 6 months) |

**Annual quota**: 120 SRLs

**Example**:
- 130 SRLs generated (108% of quota)
- 110 accepted by sales = ¥1,100K
- 45 qualified = ¥675K
- 12 closed deals = ¥600K
- **Total commission**: ¥2,375K (exceeds OTE target of ¥1,500K due to high quality)

### 5.3 For Account Managers (Renewals + Upsell)

**Split compensation**:
- 60% weighted on net retention
- 40% weighted on upsell/expansion

**Net retention calculation**:
- Start: ¥100M ARR book of business
- Renewals: ¥92M (8% churn)
- Net retention: 92%

**Commission**:
- Net retention ≥95%: Full commission (60% of target)
- Net retention 90-94%: Prorated
- Net retention <90%: Reduced commission

**Upsell commission**: 8% of net new ARR (upsell/cross-sell)

### 5.4 For Sales Management

**Sales Director**: 70% team quota, 30% individual contribution

**VP Sales**: 100% based on company new business revenue target

**Tiered similar to AE** but lower rates (reflecting less direct sales activity)

## 6. Deal Credit and Splits

### 6.1 Standard Credit

**Primary AE**: 100% credit for deals in their territory

**Territory defined by**:
- Company size
- Geography
- Industry vertical (in some cases)

### 6.2 Split Deals

**When deal involves multiple AEs**:
- Documented agreement before deal closes
- Typical splits: 50/50 or 60/40 (based on contribution)
- VP Sales arbitrates disputes

**Example scenarios**:
- Multi-location deal spanning territories
- Strategic deal requiring team effort
- Transfer mid-sales cycle

### 6.3 SDR Credit

**SDR receives lead credit** if:
- SDR generated the lead
- Lead converts to opportunity within 30 days
- AE accepts lead as SRL (Sales-Ready Lead)

**SDR credit timeline**: Up to 6 months from SRL acceptance

### 6.4 Transfers and Reassignments

**If account transferred**:
- Original AE: Credit for renewals of deals they closed (for 12 months)
- New AE: Credit for new business and renewals after 12 months

**If AE leaves company**:
- Open opportunities: Reassigned to another AE (full credit)
- Closed deals in pipeline: Original AE receives commission (paid out)

## 7. Commission Payment

### 7.1 Payment Timing

**80% paid on booking**: When deal is closed and contract signed

**20% paid on cash collection**: When customer payment received (within 45 days)

**Rationale**: Aligns with working capital management (DC_RAJ_005)
- Incentivizes sales to care about payment terms
- Reduces bad debt risk

**Example**:
- Deal closes: ¥10M ARR
- Commission: ¥1,000K (10% rate)
- Paid immediately: ¥800K
- Paid on collection (within 45 days): ¥200K

### 7.2 Payment Schedule

**Monthly commission runs**:
- Closes through last day of month
- Commission calculated: 1st-3rd of following month
- Payment: 15th of following month

**Example**: Deal closes September 25 → Commission paid October 15

### 7.3 New Hire Ramp

**Commission during ramp period**:

| Month | Quota | Commission Rate |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| Month 1-2 | 0% (training) | Draw against future (see below) |
| Month 3 | 50% of full quota | Full commission rate |
| Month 4-5 | 75% of full quota | Full commission rate |
| Month 6+ | 100% of full quota | Full commission rate |

**Draw**: Months 1-2, new hire receives guaranteed ¥500K/month (recoverable from future commissions)

## 8. Clawbacks and Adjustments

### 8.1 Commission Clawback

**Clawback occurs if**:
- Customer cancels within 90 days (full clawback)
- Customer non-payment (proportional clawback)
- Fraudulent deal (full clawback + termination)
- Material misrepresentation (full clawback)

**Process**:
- Finance notifies sales rep and manager
- Amount deducted from future commissions
- If no future commissions, repayment required

### 8.2 Deal Adjustments

**Downward adjustments**:
- Discount exceeds policy (commission on net)
- Deal size reduced before signing
- Payment terms extended (reduces 20% cash component)

**Upward adjustments**:
- Customer expands before signing
- Multi-year commitment (see Section 9.2)

### 8.3 Policy Violations

**Commission reduction/forfeiture** for:
- Unauthorized discounts
- Side agreements not approved
- Misrepresenting deal terms
- Collusion with customer

**See**: Discount Policy (POLICY-FIN-001) for approval requirements

## 9. SPIFFs and Accelerators

### 9.1 Quarterly SPIFFs

**SPIFF** = Sales Performance Incentive Fund

**Typical SPIFFs** (varies by quarter):
- First deal of quarter: ¥200K bonus
- Largest deal of quarter: ¥300K bonus
- Most SRLs (SDR): ¥150K bonus
- Product-specific (e.g., TalentHub): 2x commission

**Announced**: Start of each quarter

**Paid**: End of quarter

### 9.2 Multi-Year Deal Accelerator

**Bonus for multi-year contracts**:
- 2-year contract: +10% commission
- 3-year contract: +20% commission
- 5-year contract: +30% commission

**Rationale**: Reduces churn, improves cash flow, long-term customer relationships

**Example**:
- 1-year ¥10M deal: ¥1,000K commission (10% rate)
- 3-year ¥30M deal: ¥3,600K commission (12% rate due to +20% bonus)

### 9.3 President's Club

**Top 20% performers** invited to annual President's Club trip

**Criteria**:
- ≥120% of quota
- High customer satisfaction (NPS >70 for their deals)
- Policy compliance (no violations)

**Reward**:
- 3-day trip (destination announced annually)
- Spouse/partner invited
- Company pays all expenses
- Recognition and awards

## 10. Performance Management

### 10.1 Performance Tiers

**Based on annual quota attainment**:

| Tier | Quota Attainment | Performance Rating |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Exceeds | ≥120% | Exceeds Expectations |
| Achieves | 90-119% | Meets Expectations |
| Developing | 70-89% | Below Expectations |
| Underperforming | <70% | Performance Improvement Plan |

### 10.2 Performance Reviews

**Quarterly reviews**:
- Quota progress
- Pipeline health
- Activity metrics
- Customer satisfaction

**Annual reviews**:
- Full year performance
- Compensation adjustments
- Quota for next year
- Development goals

### 10.3 Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

**Triggered by**:
- <70% quota attainment for 2 consecutive quarters
- <50% quota attainment in any quarter
- Policy violations
- Customer complaints

**PIP duration**: 90 days

**Support provided**:
- Weekly 1:1s with manager
- Sales coaching
- Pipeline review
- Adjusted targets (if appropriate)

**Outcomes**:
- Success: Return to normal status
- Partial improvement: Extended PIP
- No improvement: Termination

## 11. Special Situations

### 11.1 Leave of Absence

**Short-term leave** (<30 days):
- Quota not adjusted
- Commission for closed deals paid normally

**Extended leave** (>30 days):
- Quota prorated
- Territory may be temporarily reassigned
- Commission for deals in progress: Split with covering AE

### 11.2 Termination

**Voluntary resignation**:
- Commissions for closed deals: Paid
- Open opportunities: Reassigned
- No clawback of past commissions

**Termination for cause**:
- Commissions for closed deals: Forfeited
- Open opportunities: Reassigned
- Clawback of recent commissions (if policy violations)

**Layoff/reduction in force**:
- Commissions for closed deals: Paid
- Severance per employment law
- Open opportunities: Reassigned

### 11.3 Promotions

**Promoted to management**:
- Switch to management compensation plan
- Existing pipeline: Continue to receive commission
- Transition period: 30 days

**Promoted to senior AE**:
- Higher quota
- Same commission structure
- May include signing bonus

## 12. Transparency and Communication

### 12.1 Commission Statements

**Monthly statements** show:
- Deals closed
- Commission earned
- Quota progress (QTD and YTD)
- Year-to-date earnings
- Next payment amount

**Access**: Sales reps can view anytime via sales portal

### 12.2 Commission Disputes

**If sales rep disagrees with commission**:
1. Review commission statement
2. Discuss with sales manager
3. If unresolved, escalate to VP Sales
4. Final decision: CFO

**Resolution timeline**: 14 days

### 12.3 Plan Changes

**Annual plan updates**:
- Announced: November (for following year)
- Feedback period: 2 weeks
- Final plan: December
- Effective: January 1

**Mid-year changes**: Rare, only for significant market shifts (requires CEO approval)

## 13. Compliance and Ethics

### 13.1 Ethical Sales Practices

**Prohibited**:
- Misrepresenting product capabilities
- Making unauthorized commitments
- Bribing customers or prospects
- Collusion with customers (fake deals)
- Hiding deal terms from management

**Consequences**: Commission forfeiture + termination

### 13.2 Discount Policy Compliance

**Sales reps must follow** Discount Policy (POLICY-FIN-001):
- Obtain proper approvals
- Document justifications
- No side agreements

**Violations**: Commission adjustment + disciplinary action

See DC_AKIKO_001 and DC_RAJ_002 for discount decision precedents

### 13.3 Reporting Violations

**To report unethical behavior**:
- Manager or VP Sales
- HR (people@nexatech.jp)
- Anonymous hotline: 0120-XXX-XXXX

**Whistleblower protection**: No retaliation for good-faith reporting
