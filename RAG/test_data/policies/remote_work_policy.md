## 1. Purpose

This policy defines NexaTech's approach to remote and flexible work to:
- Enable work-life balance and employee well-being
- Maintain productivity and collaboration
- Support diverse working styles
- Comply with labor regulations

---

## 2. Philosophy

NexaTech believes in **"work from anywhere within Japan"** with the following principles:
- **Flexibility first**: Trust employees to work where they're most effective
- **Results matter**: Focus on outcomes, not hours or location
- **Collaboration required**: Balance individual flexibility with team needs
- **Equal treatment**: Remote employees have same opportunities as office-based

---

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Eligible Roles

**Fully Remote Eligible**:
- Engineering (all roles)
- Product (Product Managers, Designers)
- Marketing (Content, Digital Marketing, Analytics)
- Customer Success (CSMs, Support)
- Finance (FP&A, Accounting)

**Hybrid Eligible** (2-3 days office per week):
- Sales (needs customer meeting flexibility)
- Executive team (strategic collaboration)
- People Operations (employee support)

**Office-Based**:
- Reception/Office Operations
- IT Support (on-site requirements)

### 3.2 Employee Requirements

To be eligible for full remote work:
- ✓ Completed 3-month probation period
- ✓ Performance rating "Meets Expectations" or higher
- ✓ Role eligible per Section 3.1
- ✓ Manager approval
- ✓ Appropriate home workspace (Section 6)

---

## 4. Work Arrangements

### 4.1 Full-Time Remote

- Work from home or other location within Japan
- Required to visit Tokyo office once per month (travel reimbursed)
- Must attend quarterly team gatherings (travel + accommodation reimbursed)
- Core hours: 10:00-15:00 JST (available for meetings)

### 4.2 Hybrid

- Minimum 2 days/week in office (team decides which days)
- Flexible which days based on meetings/collaboration needs
- Core hours: 10:00-16:00 on office days

### 4.3 Office-Based with Flexibility

- Primary work location: Tokyo office
- Can work remotely 1 day/week with manager approval
- Flexible start times (7:00-10:00 arrival)

---

## 5. Work Hours and Availability

### 5.1 Core Hours

All employees must be available during **core hours**:
- **Core hours**: 10:00-15:00 JST (5 hours)
- Respond to Slack/email within 2 hours during core hours
- Attend scheduled meetings during core hours

### 5.2 Flexible Hours

Outside core hours, employees may:
- Start early (7:00-10:00) or finish late (after 19:00)
- Take breaks for personal needs (childcare, appointments)
- Adjust schedule for optimal productivity

**Total work expectation**: 8 hours/day, 40 hours/week

### 5.3 Overtime

Remote employees eligible for overtime pay same as office employees:
- Overtime requires manager pre-approval
- Tracked via time-tracking system
- Compensated per labor law

---

## 6. Home Workspace Requirements

### 6.1 Minimum Standards

Remote employees must have:
- ☐ Dedicated workspace (separate from personal areas)
- ☐ Ergonomic desk and chair
- ☐ Reliable high-speed internet (≥50 Mbps)
- ☐ Quiet environment for calls/meetings
- ☐ Appropriate lighting

### 6.2 Company-Provided Equipment

NexaTech provides:
- Laptop (MacBook Pro or equivalent)
- External monitor (24" or 27")
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset with microphone
- Laptop stand
- Webcam (if needed)

### 6.3 Home Office Stipend

**One-time setup**: ¥100,000 for:
- Desk and ergonomic chair
- Lighting
- Office supplies

**Monthly stipend**: ¥5,000 for:
- Internet costs
- Electricity
- Phone/mobile data

Submit expenses via expense system with receipts.

## 7. Communication and Collaboration

### 7.1 Required Tools

- **Slack**: Primary communication (check every 2 hours during core hours)
- **Zoom**: Video meetings (camera ON for team meetings)
- **WorkFlow Pro**: Project management and collaboration
- **Email**: External communication and formal notices

### 7.2 Meeting Etiquette

**For all remote participants**:
- ✓ Camera ON for team meetings (unless bandwidth issues)
- ✓ Mute when not speaking
- ✓ Use virtual backgrounds if home environment distracting
- ✓ Be on time (within 2 minutes)

**For meeting organizers**:
- ✓ Record meetings for those who can't attend
- ✓ Share notes and action items post-meeting
- ✓ Schedule during core hours when possible

### 7.3 Response Time Expectations

| Channel | Expected Response Time |
|---------|----------------------|
| Slack (urgent) | 30 minutes during core hours |
| Slack (normal) | 2 hours during core hours |
| Email | 24 hours (business days) |
| Phone call | Immediate (if available) |

**Status indicators**:
- 🟢 Available: Respond within 30 min
- 🟡 Busy: Respond within 2 hours
- 🔴 Do Not Disturb: Emergency only
- ⚫ Away: Not available (use calendar)

## 8. Performance and Productivity

### 8.1 Performance Standards

Remote employees held to **same performance standards** as office employees:
- Evaluated on outcomes and deliverables
- Regular 1:1s with manager (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Quarterly performance reviews
- Annual goal setting

### 8.2 Productivity Monitoring

NexaTech does **NOT** use:
- ❌ Surveillance software
- ❌ Keystroke monitoring
- ❌ Screenshot tools
- ❌ Activity trackers

We **DO** use:
- ✓ Project deliverables and milestones
- ✓ Meeting attendance
- ✓ Code commits (engineers)
- ✓ Self-reported time (for compliance)

**Trust-based model**: We hire adults and treat them as such.

---

## 9. Security and Data Protection

### 9.1 Data Security Requirements

Remote employees must:
- ☐ Use company VPN when accessing internal systems
- ☐ Enable full-disk encryption on company devices
- ☐ Use strong passwords + 2FA for all accounts
- ☐ Lock computer when stepping away
- ☐ Never share login credentials
- ☐ Report security incidents immediately

### 9.2 Prohibited Actions

- ❌ Working in public places (cafes, co-working) when handling sensitive data
- ❌ Using public WiFi without VPN
- ❌ Allowing family/friends to use company devices
- ❌ Storing company data on personal devices
- ❌ Discussing confidential matters in public

See Information Security Policy (POLICY-IT-001) for full requirements.

## 10. Remote Work from Abroad (Temporary)

### 10.1 Short-Term (≤14 days)

Employees may work remotely from abroad for ≤14 days with:
- Manager approval (5 days advance notice)
- Stable internet and workspace
- Consideration of time zone differences

**Not considered**: Business travel or vacation (separate policies apply)

### 10.2 Extended (15-90 days)

Requires additional approvals:
- Manager + HR + Legal approval
- 30 days advance notice
- Tax implications review
- Labor law compliance check
- Time zone compatibility plan

**Limitations**:
- Must work ≥4 hours overlap with Tokyo core hours
- Limited to 90 days per year total
- Not available during probation period

### 10.3 Permanent Relocation Abroad

Not permitted under current policy. Requires:
- Case-by-case approval from CEO
- Legal entity in target country or EOR arrangement
- Salary adjustment to local market

## 11. Team Gatherings and Office Visits

### 11.1 Quarterly Team Offsites

All remote employees required to attend:
- Frequency: Once per quarter (4x per year)
- Duration: 2-3 days
- Location: Tokyo or regional office
- Company covers: Travel, hotel, meals

**Purpose**: Team bonding, strategic planning, face-to-face collaboration

### 11.2 Monthly Office Visits

Full-time remote employees expected to visit Tokyo office:
- Frequency: Once per month minimum
- Duration: 1-2 days
- Company covers: Travel (if outside Tokyo area)

**Purpose**: Maintain connection, casual collaboration, use office resources

### 11.3 Optional Office Time

Office always available for remote employees:
- Book desk via office management system
- Use meeting rooms for client calls
- Access on-site amenities (gym, cafe)

---

## 12. Equipment and Support

### 12.1 IT Support

Remote employees receive same IT support as office employees:
- **Help desk**: it-support@nexatech.jp (response within 4 hours)
- **Hardware issues**: Ship to office for repair/replacement (2-3 day turnaround)
- **Emergency**: Loaner equipment available

### 12.2 Equipment Replacement

- **Laptops**: 3-year cycle (or sooner if performance issues)
- **Monitors**: 5-year cycle
- **Accessories**: As needed (request via IT)

### 12.3 Returning Equipment

If leaving company or changing arrangement:
- Return all company equipment within 14 days
- Ship via provided prepaid label
- Failure to return: Deducted from final paycheck

## 13. Manager Responsibilities

### 13.1 For Remote Team Members

Managers must:
- ✓ Schedule regular 1:1s (at least bi-weekly)
- ✓ Set clear goals and expectations
- ✓ Provide regular feedback
- ✓ Include remote employees in team decisions
- ✓ Be flexible about working hours (within reason)
- ✓ Address performance issues promptly

### 13.2 Team Building

Managers responsible for:
- Creating inclusive team culture
- Organizing virtual team activities
- Ensuring remote employees aren't excluded from opportunities
- Facilitating communication between remote and office team members

---

## 14. Remote Work Agreement

### 14.1 Required Documentation

Remote employees must sign Remote Work Agreement covering:
- Workspace setup and safety
- Data security commitments
- Equipment care
- Tax and legal acknowledgments
- Understanding of policy terms

### 14.2 Annual Renewal

Remote work arrangements reviewed annually:
- Performance assessment
- Role changes
- Business needs
- Employee preferences

**Not guaranteed**: Remote work is privilege, not right. Can be revoked if:
- Performance declines
- Role requirements change
- Business needs require office presence
- Policy violations

---

## 15. Special Circumstances

### 15.1 Temporary Remote Work (Non-Standard)

Short-term remote work for special circumstances:
- **Medical**: Doctor's note required, manager approval
- **Family care**: Up to 10 days per year, manager approval
- **Home repairs/emergencies**: Manager discretion

### 15.2 Parents and Caregivers

Additional flexibility for parents/caregivers:
- Flexible start/end times (within 7:00-20:00)
- Split shifts (work morning + evening, break during day)
- Reduced hours options (consult HR)

### 15.3 Trial Period for New Hires

New employees start:
- First 3 months: Office-based (3-5 days/week)
- After probation: Eligible for full remote (if role eligible)

**Rationale**: In-person onboarding builds relationships and culture

---

## 16. Hybrid Work Schedule Coordination

### 16.1 Team Core Days

Each team designates 2 "core days" for in-office:
- Entire team present these days
- Schedule collaborative work, meetings, workshops
- Builds team cohesion

### 16.2 Booking System

Use office management system to:
- Reserve desk (hot-desking for hybrid employees)
- Book meeting rooms
- See who's in office (encourage serendipitous collaboration)
