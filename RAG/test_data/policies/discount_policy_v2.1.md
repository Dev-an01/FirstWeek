## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for pricing discounts on NexaTech products to ensure:
- Consistent pricing across customer segments
- Sustainable gross margins (target: 70%+)
- Fair treatment of all customers
- Clear approval workflows for exceptions

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All NexaTech products (WorkFlow Pro, TeamSync, TalentHub)
- New customer acquisitions
- Renewal negotiations
- Upsell/cross-sell transactions
- All sales team members and executives

---

## 3. Standard Discount Tiers

### 3.1 New Customer Discounts

| Customer Tier | Annual Contract Value | Max Discount | Approval Required |
|--------------|----------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| SMB (1-99 employees) | ¥1M - ¥5M | 10% | AE + Sales Manager |
| Mid-Market (100-499) | ¥5M - ¥15M | 15% | AE + Sales Director |
| Enterprise (500-1999) | ¥15M - ¥30M | 20% | VP Sales |
| Strategic (2000+) | ¥30M+ | 25% | VP Sales + CFO |

### 3.2 Renewal Discounts

| Situation | Max Discount | Approval Required |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| On-time renewal (no churn risk) | 5% | AE |
| At-risk customer (churn signals) | 15% | VP Sales |
| Strategic account negotiation | 20% | VP Sales + CFO |
| Competitive threat (documented) | 25% | VP Sales + CFO + CEO |

### 3.3 Volume Discounts

Automatically applied for multi-year contracts:
- 2-year commitment: +5% discount
- 3-year commitment: +10% discount
- 5-year commitment: +15% discount

---

## 4. Prohibited Discounts

The following are **NOT PERMITTED** without CEO approval:
- Discounts >25% for any customer
- Discounts applied retroactively
- Discounts without documented business justification
- Free product giveaways (exceptions: NFP/education sector)
- Discounts that bring gross margin below 65%

---

## 5. Approval Workflow

### 5.1 Standard Process

1. **AE prepares discount request** with business justification including:
   - Customer profile and strategic value
   - Competitive landscape and pricing
   - Expected lifetime value (LTV)
   - Risk assessment if discount is denied

2. **Required approvals** based on tier (see Section 3.1-3.2)

3. **Finance review** for discounts >15%:
   - Gross margin impact analysis
   - CAC payback period calculation
   - LTV:CAC ratio validation (must be >3:1)

4. **Documentation** in Salesforce with:
   - Approval chain (digital signatures)
   - Business justification
   - Competitive intelligence (if applicable)

### 5.2 Expedited Approval (Emergency)

For time-sensitive deals requiring >20% discount:
- VP Sales and CFO can approve jointly via Slack/email
- Must be followed by formal documentation within 24 hours
- Limited to 5 uses per quarter per sales team

---

## 6. Special Situations

### 6.1 Nonprofit and Education

- Eligible organizations: 30% standard discount
- Requires verification (501(c)(3) or equivalent)
- Annual contract minimum: ¥500K
- Approval: VP Sales

### 6.2 Strategic Partnerships

- Partner/reseller discounts: 20-40% based on volume commitment
- Requires partnership agreement
- Approval: CEO + CFO

### 6.3 Startup Programs

- Early-stage startups (<50 employees, <$1M funding): 50% discount for year 1
- Graduates to standard pricing year 2
- Limited to 20 startups per year
- Approval: CMO (lead generation program)

---

## 7. Value-Based Alternatives to Discounts

**Preferred approach**: Offer value-adds instead of price reductions when possible.

### 7.1 Approved Value-Adds (cost to deliver vs customer perceived value)

| Value-Add | Cost to NexaTech | Customer Value | Approval |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|----------|
| Dedicated CSM | ¥800K/year | ¥3M perceived | VP CS |
| Premium support (4hr SLA) | ¥500K/year | ¥2M perceived | VP CS |
| Custom integrations (3) | ¥1.2M one-time | ¥5M perceived | CTO |
| Advanced analytics module | ¥300K/year | ¥2M perceived | VP Product |
| Extended training (5 days) | ¥600K one-time | ¥1.5M perceived | VP CS |
| Executive business reviews (quarterly) | ¥400K/year | ¥1M perceived | VP Sales |

**Strategic value**: Value-adds cost less than cash discounts but have higher perceived value. Preferred for strategic accounts.

## 9. Competitive Price Matching

### 9.1 Policy

NexaTech does **NOT** automatically match competitor pricing. We compete on value, not price.

### 9.2 Evaluation Criteria

If customer presents competitive offer, evaluate:
1. **Is the offer genuine?** (Request written quote)
2. **Feature parity?** (Ensure apples-to-apples comparison)
3. **Strategic value of customer?** (Lifetime value assessment)
4. **Margin impact?** (Can we match and maintain 65% margin?)

If all criteria met, VP Sales + CFO can approve competitive match up to 25% discount.

## 10. Precedent Cases

### 10.1 MegaCorp Japan Renewal (DC_AKIKO_001)

**Situation**: Strategic customer (¥25M ARR) requested 20% renewal discount citing competitive offer.

**Decision**: Approved 15% discount + ¥3M value-adds (dedicated CSM, premium features, custom integrations) structured as 3-year contract.

**Rationale**: 
- Stayed within 15% policy for renewals
- Value-adds cost ¥800K but perceived as ¥5M value
- 3-year commitment reduced churn risk
- Effective discount ~17% vs 20% requested
- Protected gross margin (68% maintained)

**Outcome**: Customer accepted, signed 3-year deal, later expanded by 500 seats.

**Precedent**: This approach is now template for strategic account renewals >¥20M.

## 11. Policy Exceptions

### 11.1 When to Request Exception

Situations requiring CEO approval (outside policy boundaries):
- Discount >25% for any reason
- Discounts that reduce gross margin below 65%
- Government/enterprise customers with unique requirements
- Market entry pricing for new regions

### 11.2 Exception Request Process

1. VP Sales prepares detailed business case including:
   - Why exception is necessary
   - Long-term strategic value
   - Financial impact analysis (3-year projection)
   - Precedent risk assessment

2. Present to CEO + CFO in joint meeting

3. If approved, document rationale and set expiration date

4. Exceptions reviewed quarterly to prevent policy erosion

---

## 12. Sales Compensation Impact

Sales commission is calculated on **net revenue** (after discounts):
- Incentivizes sales team to minimize discounts
- Higher commission % on deals with <10% discount
- Standard commission on deals with 10-20% discount
- Reduced commission on deals with >20% discount

See Sales Compensation Policy (POLICY-SALES-001) for details.

---

## 13. Policy Violations

### 13.1 Consequences

- **Unauthorized discount applied**: Deal subject to retrospective review, sales credit may be adjusted
- **Repeated violations**: Performance improvement plan
- **Intentional circumvention**: Termination

### 13.2 Reporting Violations

Finance team monitors all deals. Violations reported to VP Sales and CFO monthly.
